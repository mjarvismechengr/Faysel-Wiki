### Events

Date: 36th (night) - 38th (mid-day) of [[Iskel]]

| Scenes | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Camp & The Road | The party made camp and strung up the wendigo as a warning to passersby and potential enemies. Jaren Tucker, a fur trapper, appeared in the wilderness and sold an owlbear taxidermy hat to Rex and a fox fur vest to Lucian. The party discussed the [[Ruins of Mirskwa]] and agreed to avoid them. The area is full of powerful "abominations." Little is known about [[Hogaak]], though more learned individuals (particularly wizards) may have deeper knowledge of it. The party decided to head toward [[Y'varius]]. |
| Wizard's Tower | Approaching a tower hidden within the forest, the party activated a trap, opening a portal to the [[Abyss]] accompanied by a foul smell. A massive demon known as a Barlgura leapt out and attacked. Lucian's Hound of Ill Omen defeated the Barlgura with a jugular strike, and the creature dissolved into black ichor, but moments later it reappeared on the other side of the still open portal. The party realized they needed to close the portal. Altair cast _Moonbeam_ and closed the portal within a single round. |
| Tower Entry Hall | The party entered through the front door of the tower and each vanished into thin air. They arrived in a circular room etched with runes, featuring 11 archways, each marked with a different symbol: sun, vertical lines, scroll, skull, droplet, crescent moon, three boxes in an L shape, wave, spiral, X, wand casting a spell. Rex, Bardock, Altair, and Dotrice went through the scroll portal while Bodhi and Lucian entered the wave portal.|
| Wizard's Study | The scroll portal led to A large chamber lined with bookshelves, art pieces encased in glass, and a work station. One section of wall was covered in diagrams and notes near a desk. A small marble bench with dark liquid dripping from it actually turned out to be an shrine to [[Daghor Malatok]]. The ceiling resembled the current night sky. There was also a door leading back to the entry hall. |
| Drowning Trap | The wave symboled portal led to a smooth stone chamber with no visible return portal. Water began flooding the chamber. Bodhi spotted the outline of a hatch in the ceiling. The two made it through the hatch into a crawl space with a return portal, escaping back to the main chamber before they drowned. |
| Laboratory | After returning to the entry hall, Bodhi and Lucian entered the Skull portal next. The room they arrived in smelled of organic rot and alchemical substances, with signs of repeated explosions. There were several surgery tables with live subjects and two doors, one standard, and one marked "Do Not Open." |
