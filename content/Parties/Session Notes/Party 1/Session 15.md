### Events

Date: 38th (mid-day) - 38th (evening) of [[Iskel]]

| Scenes | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| The Grand Study | **Art Displays:** [[Rex Tinker]] and [[Dotrice]] investigate the art displays. <br/><br/> - _Harbinger of Doom_: A formal portrait of a handsome knight in polished golden armor stands proudly framed. His expression is resolute, his posture unyielding. Across his chest are **seven insignias**, each one distinct, each one meticulously rendered and prominently displayed. _“Even the righteous carry the weight of what is to come.”_ (Symbols recognized as those at [[Glass Lake]], having something to do with [[Varethis]]). <br/><br/> - _Thane of Ruin_: This painting shows a robed figure standing amidst ruin, its form crowned by **three skulls**, each turned slightly outward as if observing from its own perspective. _"Power Shared is not Diminished.”_ <br/><br/> - _Massacre Eternal_: The painting depicts a battlefield consumed by chaos — an overwhelming tide of undead crashing forward. At the center of it all stands a **single towering balor**, its shadowy form wreathed in flame, failing to resist the tide alone as it is slowly dragged down. _“Even the Mighty are Buried Underneath Inevitability.”_ <br/><br/> - _Echo of Hunger_: Suspended within the glass is a crude necklace of teeth and bone fragments, strung together with dark cord. Most of the teeth are dull and aged, but **seven** gleam brightly, worked in gold, each polished to a mirror shine.  _“Desire does not diminish with death.”_ <br/><br/> - _Fallen Sovereign_: The massive skull of a black dragon rests upon a stone pedestal, its surface worn but intact. **Two great horns** curve outward from its crown, symmetrical and unbroken, framing the empty sockets where its eyes once burned with life. _“All Rulers, in time, are reduced to Silence.”_ <br/><br/> - _Mortal Engine_: Suspended in a clear fluid, a human heart floats in perfect preservation. It pulses with unnatural rhythm, a steady sequence of **nine beats** before a brief stillness… and then the pattern repeats. _“Life is not sacred; it is a mechanism.”_ <br/><br/> - _Threnody_: A sculpted stone bust of a woman rests atop a pedestal, her face frozen in silent agony. From her **two eyes**, dark crimson fluid flows in thin, continuous streams, tracing paths down her cheeks before vanishing into the basin below. _“Grief endures all.”_ <br/><br/>Several letter sequences and names were recovered throughout the exploration of the above artifacts, their meaning yet to be fully deciphered – but the party believes they answer a codex within the wall of the Study. They also believe the letters are letters forming the Wizard’s name. <br/> - Letters: R, R, A, A, M, I, H <br/> - Phrases: _"I am Harr"_, _"I am Rarh"_, _"I am Ahrr"_ (none worked) <br/><br/><br/>**Bookshelves, Desk, Orrery, and Wall Notes:** [[Altair Flickerfoot]] looked over several areas in the study, finding several books on the bookshelves. He also found a clue about the subject of the wizard's recent study, [[Wandering Graveyard]]. He only gave the desk a cursory glance. |
| The Laboratory | While half the party was investigating the study, [[Bodhi Orsimer]] and [[Lucian]] happened upon the wizard's laboratory <br/><br/>**Surgical Tables:** On the surgical tables, they discovered a half-zombified elf and a grotesque mass of stitched-together limbs. The latter, still sentient and begging for death, was granted mercy by Bohdi. This act of compassion caused a fundamental shift in Bohdi’s soul, aligning him toward Chaotic Good. <br/><br/><br/> **Menagerie:** in a room full of stasis tubes, two living creatures were left within. <br/><br/> - Clearing the fog in the first stasis tube revealed a one-eyed, sharp-toothed entity that spoke telepathically. It mocked the adventurers, claiming knowledge of their pasts—specifically calling out Lucian’s burned rune and questioning Bohdi’s "heroic" history before the pits. Lucian and Bodhi felt unnerved and dismissed it to an unknown location. <br/><br/> - The second tube held a creature with a humanoid form, a tiger’s head, and reversed hands. The creature, [[Savu]], tired of being a test subject, offered his services as a broker. A blood contract, signed in black ichor by Savu, and blood by lucian and bodhi was struck: Savu will direct one of his contacts with knowledge of [[Hogaak]] to the party and promises not to harm the party directly in exchange for his release. <br/><br/><br/>**Elsewhere in the Lab:** while investigating a summoning room, the pair of adventurers was able to deduce that Savu was a denizen of the [[Nine Hells of Baator]], a type of devil known as a rakshasa. Lucian and Bodhi weren't able to deduce the required ingredient for a partially constructed potion, so it was ruined. They accidentally released a goat, which damaged the alchemy portion of the lab. They were able to gather a few potions. |
| Prison Trap | Once regrouped in the entry hall, the party went through the portal with the symbol of of vertical lines, which teleported Dotrice, Rex, Bohdi, and Altair into the laboratory’s holding tubes. Within the entry hall, the lit illusory bar on the floor, increased to two bars and shifted from blue to yellow. Lucian, with the help of his wolf, successfully operated the control panel to release them. |
| Poison Gas Trap | Passing through the droplet symbol portal led to a chamber with a triggered yellowish-green chemical gas that burned throats and eyes. The entry hall's yellow bars grew to 3 and shifted to orange. The party was forced to break through a wall to escape back to the main hub. |
| Living Quarters | The party eventually breached the upper living quarters, a chamber that felt lived-in but recently abandoned. The commenced searching for the name of the Wizard to hopefully solve the Codex. <br/><br/>Notable Discoveries: <br/><br/> - The Prophetic Painting: A depiction of a rotting tower led by a wizard and an army of undead, flanked by the Knight and the Three-Skulled Thane from the study. <br/><br/> - The Mirror of Scrying: An ornate, unbreakable mirror. Identification revealed it is currently active—someone is watching the party through it. |
