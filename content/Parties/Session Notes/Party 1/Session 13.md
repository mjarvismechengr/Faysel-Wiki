### Events

Date: 32nd - 35th of [[Iskel]]

| Scenes | Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| [[Bardock's Awakening Site]] | The group traveled from [[Lucian's Cabin]] to [[Bardock's Awakening Site]]. They happened upon a path [[Bardock 1563]] felt he's traveled before. The party comes upon a mass grave site of warforged. [[Rex Tinker]] finds old tech that can be harvested from the fallen. Each warforged has a runic symbol marking them as coming from [[Aeranas]]. Among the remains, the writing "RETROFIT INITIATIVE - BLACK LANCE" was found on one of the warforged. Other clues found were necromantic scars indicating a fight with a powerful undead. All this means that bardock now knows he's from [[Aeranas]] during the [[Age of Wonder]], at some point was retrofit, and he was part of a team that fought a powerful undead. Rex and Arthur harvested a functioning power core and a non-functioning gatling attachment. |
| [[Mt. Storm King]] | The party saw switchbacks heading up the northern foothills of the mountain but decided to proceed east instead of going up. They ran into gas spore fungus as they pressed on. |
| [[Glass Lake]] | The party discovered a lake with a glassy top that doesn't move or ripple despite any outside action. The reflections in the lake did not mirror reality. Once several things within the reflection were made to mirror reality, a large pillar with a platform on top ascended from the water. They climbed up the staircase spiraling around the pillar to find a central dais and 6 pedestals in a ring along the outer edge of the platform. Each of the pillars had a puzzle to be solved and they completed 4. As each puzzle was solved, that pedestal sunk into the floor of the platform and a pillar rose from the water on the edge of the lake. [[Dotrice]] made an oath to uphold the law. |
| Eastward | The party continued moving east toward the [[Ruins of Mirskwa]] and on their way encounterd a ring of standing stones. They were attacked by a wendigo with a sword in its back, which was killed by [[Altair Flickerfoot]]. The sword couldn't be pulled free, but it could be cut out of the back. It was quickly recognized as the long missing sword, [[Swift Justice]]. |
