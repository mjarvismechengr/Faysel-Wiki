| Cult Ritual                     | While sneaking around, [[Arthur Mourhan]] happened upon the cult leader, [[Zalryr]] performing a ritual to extract the secrets from a victim while another lays motionless in the corner. He said “You _saw_ it. The ruin beneath the obsidian falls. The key is there. Show me.” While dispatching cultists and shadows, Zalryr was able to complete the ritual and extracted the secret visions from the victim. Since Bodhi was near, he also saw a stone map etched into a cavern wall, A massive iron door with seven keyholes nestled at the heart of a ruin carved from a glacier, A tree split in half, standing alone at the center of a blasted battlefield, with bones half-buried around its roots, A woman with a burned-out eye whispering, “[[Varethis]].” After the ritual, Zalryr surrendered and the party tortured him for information. They cut off his hand, fed him his own fingers, and [[Lucian]] used the suggestion spell which yielded a thick, black, heavy iron key with an intricate runes and teeth. The handle matches the 6 pronged crown of the [[Hallwinter Family]] crest, though with one prong broken off. |
