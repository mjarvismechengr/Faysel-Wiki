## Overview

The Candelabra is the most influential magical organization in the known world, governing the study, application, and even in some ways the regulation of magic. It presents itself as a neutral institution dedicated to advancing magical knowledge, mediating political disputes, and preventing the misuse of dangerous magic. Its influence reaches into the courts of kings, the research of scholars, and the clandestine dealings of powerful factions.

The guild is composed of nine Archmages, each overseeing a distinct realm of magical expertise, and their respective towers spread across the world. The tenth and greatest of these towers, [[Ixaghor]], serves as the guild’s headquarters and seat of power.

Though the Candelabra does not officially govern, its subtle influence guides and shapes the world, ensuring that no one, mage or monarch, wields power unchecked. While some within the guild genuinely believe in protecting civilization, others use their position to hoard knowledge, manipulate events, and serve their own ambitions.

## Culture

The Candelabra is a magocracy, where power and influence are tied to magical prowess, knowledge, and cunning. While all guild members, archmages included, align with the guild’s overarching goals, each has personal ambitions and is willing to maneuver, manipulate, and even undermine their peers to achieve them. The guild presents a united front to the outside world, but internally, it is a battlefield of egos, secrets, and hidden agendas.

### Philosophy & Core Beliefs 

<u>**Power Above All Else**</u>
> The Candelabra does not serve a moral cause—it serves magic and its own power. Knowledge, influence, and arcane mastery are the highest virtues. Strength determines rank, and archmages are rarely replaced unless they die or are forcibly removed. Ambition is not discouraged—it’s expected. Lower-ranked members actively vie for favor in hopes of rising through the ranks. Those who fail to meet the guild’s ever-changing standards may find themselves expelled, exiled, or quietly eliminated.

<u>**Political Neutrality**</u>
> The guild’s official stance is neutrality in world affairs, but in practice, each archmage influences nations and events in their own way. The guild is more interested in controlling the flow of knowledge and power than outright conquest.

### Recruitment & Hierarchy

The Candelabra recruits the best minds, selecting those who show promise in arcane study and political acumen. Promotion is based on merit and influence, but also on one's ability to navigate the guild’s hidden power struggles.

<u>**Ranks:**</u>
    - **Archmage (9 total):** Rule the guild and hold absolute authority.
    - **Magister:** Senior mages often entrusted with specific research, diplomatic, or teaching roles.
    - **Scholar & Adept:** Researchers and mid level mages, some of whom may one day rise to power.
    - **Magewright, Arcanist, Spellsword:** Those who use magic practically; crafting spells, wards, and enchantments, and the warrior mages of the guild.
    - **Apprentice:** Low level students learning to hone their craft and find their place in the magical world. 

### Symbols

High-ranking members wear robes embroidered with arcane sigils, but many dress discreetly to avoid unwanted attention.

> The official emblem of the Candelabra is a golden nine-branched candelabrum, symbolizing the nine archmages.
