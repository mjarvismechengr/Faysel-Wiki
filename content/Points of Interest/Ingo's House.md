## Overview 

[[Ingo Illinar]] is a respected, if enigmatic, figure in Seabrook. His house functions as a private mercenary contracting post. While the exterior suggests a utilitarian lifestyle, the inside is neat, efficient, and armed to the teeth. The front sitting room doubles as a waiting area for hopeful sellswords and shiphands looking for work. Those with more serious credentials are invited deeper inside.

Despite his secretive nature, Ingo has carved out a niche for himself: he supplies dependable crews for merchant ships, offers muscle for dangerous expeditions, and occasionally arranges private security for council members or visiting dignitaries. His knowledge of tactics, terrain, and personalities makes him invaluable, and it’s said he has at least one favor owed by every councilor, whether they admit it or not.
