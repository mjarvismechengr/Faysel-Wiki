## Overview 

Towering above the [[Endor Forest]], Mt. Storm King pierces the clouds like the blade of a god. Named in reverence to [[Iskandar]], Lord of Storms, its slopes are usually shrouded in rain, mist, and crackling skyfire. The mountain serves both as a natural landmark and a quiet test for those seeking the favor, or the attention, of the gods.
