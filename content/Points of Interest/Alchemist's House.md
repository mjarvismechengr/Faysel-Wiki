## Overview 

A few miles north of [[Seabrook]], set back from the coastal road and half hidden by scrub and wind-bent trees, stands the old Alchemist's House. The structure is modest but solid, built of weathered stone with a narrow upper floor and a sagging porch that faces the sea winds.

The house once belonged to a reclusive alchemist whose experiments sparked rumors of dark magic and the creation of alchemical gold. When he vanished roughly twenty years ago, no clear explanation followed. Over time, suspicion hardened into legend. Locals speak of strange lights in the windows, odd smells drifting on the wind, and quiet figures moving around the property long after it should have been abandoned.

In truth, the house has not stood empty. [[Sanbalet's Gang]] quietly took control of the property and now uses it as a base for their smuggling operations. Crates and contraband move through its cellar and out along discreet routes by land and sea. From the outside it remains little more than a decaying curiosity, a place most townsfolk avoid out of habit. Beneath that neglected exterior, however, it functions as an organized and profitable hub tied directly to Seabrook’s black market trade.
