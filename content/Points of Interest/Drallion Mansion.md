## Overview 

Drallion Mansion is a large merchant estate overlooking the older quarter of [[Seabrook]]. Built during a period when trade along the [[Siren Coast]] was rapidly expanding, the house was intended to project wealth and stability to visiting merchants, captains, and investors. Its limestone walls, broad balconies, and commanding position above the town reflect a time when the mansion served as both residence and symbol of commercial success.

The estate belonged for many years to the merchant [[Aubreck Drallion]], whose fortunes were tied to maritime ventures across the region. As his business declined, the house gradually fell into disrepair, its grand halls growing quiet and many of its rooms left unused. Despite this neglect, the mansion’s structure remains solid, and the grounds still hint at the status it once held within Seabrook’s mercantile circles.

Following the recovery of a valuable strongbox from the wreck of the [[Emperor of the Waves]], Drallion transferred ownership of the property to the adventuring company known as the [[Seaward Milkmen]]. With their arrival, the estate has begun to take on a new life. Though much of the mansion still requires restoration, it now serves as the group’s headquarters within Seabrook, maintained with the help of the loyal steward [[Vertheg]].
