## Overview 

The Seabrook Barracks and Jail is a sturdy, two-level structure that houses the town's holding cells as well as bunks and space for personal items of the town guard. Located on a small rise overlooking the town, it’s positioned to respond quickly to unrest on the roads, the docks, or the cliffs.

The jail in the cellar consists of two sections. A single large chamber holds drunks, brawling fishers, and other troublemakers who need to cool off for a few nights. A side passage holds six individual cells. The jail is used to hold prisoners with sentences of up to a year, with those facing longer terms or sentenced to hard labor are transferred to [[Vareth-Khul]], a large, heavily fortified prison in the far north.

It is here that Captain [[Eliander Fireborn]] maintains a skeleton force of loyal guards to keep order within Seabrook proper. Though the structure is defensive, it feels more like a tired bureaucracy than a martial fortress. Still, it has teeth, especially when outsiders stir trouble or smugglers overstep.
