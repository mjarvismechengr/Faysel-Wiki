## Overview 

A majestic, unnatural still lake lying at the eastern foot of [[Mt. Storm King]]. Its surface is perfectly flat and reflective, impervious to rain, wind, or thrown stones. 

While traveling, the party was able to uncover a set of puzzles. The first involved altering what was seen in the reflection of the lake to better mirror reality. Once done, a pillar with a circular platform at the top rose out of the center of the lake. Atop the platform is a central dais with six pedestals along the rim. Each of the pedestals required something to be done, whereupon the pedestal would sink into the floor of the platform and another pillar would rise out of the water at the lake's edge.

---

**The Dais:**
> A fading inscription is carved elegantly into the stone dais. It reads: 
> 
> “We swore six and stood as one.  
> in city, in law, in watch, in fellowship,  
> beneath our standard, and by our word.  
> So long as these endure, so endures Varethis.”


**Pedestal 1 — Oath of the City:**
> The pedestal is carved in the likeness of a small city gate. Its towers rise intact, but the arch between them is broken, the keystone absent.
> 
> Around base of the pedestal reads inscription: _“This city is our charge; its stones our law, its people our kin. In their keeping, we find our purpose.”_

Solution: Replace the keystone. Only something from Varethis, or that represents Varethis will work. No ordinary rock or stone will. 


**Pedestal 2 — Oath of the Scales:**
> A pair of tarnished bronze scales rest upon this pedestal, one pan heaped with corroded coins, the other left bare.
> 
> The scales tremble faintly, tilting hard under the weight of wealth.
> 
> An inscription along the base reads: _“We weigh all with an even hand; no coin, no crown, no kin shall tip the measure of justice.”_

Solution: Balance the scales.


**Pedestal 3 — Oath of the Watch:**
> Here stands an unlit brazier, its surface etched with sunbursts and crescents. Though dark, when you draw near it you feel a lingering warmth, as if it were last tended not long ago.
> 
> The stone bears the words: _“From dawn to dusk, and dusk to dawn, our vigil endures, that peace may rest upon these walls.”_

Solution: Light the brazier


**Pedestal 4 — Oath of Fellowship:**
> Several figures are carved in shallow relief around this pedestal, armored knights standing shoulder-to-shoulder. Yet one figure is missing, its outline eroded into a hollow gap. 
> 
> The words beneath read: _“Bound by honor, we stand together; betrayal of one is betrayal of all.”_

Solution: stand in the gap. 


**Pedestal 5 — Oath of the Standard:**
> A bare iron socket rises from this pedestal. Around it, the stone has been carved in the shape of rippling cloth.
> 
> Beneath it runs the inscription: _“The banner of Varethis shall not fall while we yet draw breath; in its shadow we lead with integrity.”_

Solution: Find or make a banner of Varethis 


**Pedestal 6 — Oath of the Word:**
> A tall stone tablet dominates this pedestal, its script eroded to near-nothing. As you draw close, a faint ringing fills your ears.
> 
> The inscription reads: _“Our word is iron; once spoken, it is law. Let no vow be broken.”_

Solution: make an oath.
