## Overview 

Tucked against the cliffside overlooking [[Old Seabrook]], The Wicker Goat is a squat, stone-walled tavern with smoke-blackened timbers and a goat-shaped weathervane. Built from the salvaged beams of a wrecked warship and older Dwarven stonework, it's the oldest surviving tavern in Seabrook and a favored haunt for off-duty guards, veterans, and anyone who’s earned their calluses.

The name comes from a half-charred wicker goat effigy that still hangs above the hearth, a relic from a centuries-old ritual once meant to ward off sea-devils. Locals now use it to hang offerings: bits of rope, iron nails, coins, or dog tags in memory of fallen comrades.
