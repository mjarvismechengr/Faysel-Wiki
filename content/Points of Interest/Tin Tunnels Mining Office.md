## Overview 

Once the estate of a long-defunct noble family, this sprawling house was sold several years ago and has since been gutted and refitted to serve as the local command center of [[Tin Tunnels Mining Co.]], the dwarven operation extracting silver and promising veins of more valuable ore from the coastal cliffs outside Seabrook.

During business hours, the halls echo with the scratching of quills and the low murmur of dwarves coordinating shipping manifests, ore processing schedules, and security patrols. At night, the building is guarded but quiet.
