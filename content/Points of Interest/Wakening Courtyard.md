## Overview 

The Wakening Courtyard lies within [[Palindrar]] on the [[Sunrise Cliffs]]. It is a shrine to [[Solara]] in remembrance of her love and the founding of the city after their narrow escape from slavery across the [[Stovlok Sea]].  A beautiful, well kept garden grows here, with statues, benches, a carved mural depicting the founding of Palindrar, and at the center of the courtyard lies a brazier. Every morning the first beam of light to touch the city strikes the brazier and ignites its flame, burning all day and until night falls upon it.
