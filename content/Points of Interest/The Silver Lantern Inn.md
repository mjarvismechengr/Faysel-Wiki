## Overview 

The Silver Lantern Inn is [[Oldport]]’s most well-known and "respectable" establishment, drawing in travelers, merchants, and adventurers alike. The exterior boasts a polished wooden facade with silver-painted lanterns hanging at the entrance, their soft glow lighting the cobbled path. 

Inside, the air is thick with the scent of roasted meats, sea salt, and expensive tobacco. A crackling fireplace and plush seating give the illusion of comfort, but the presence of off-duty city guards and observant servers ensures that no conversation stays entirely private.

The inn offers fine drinks and discreet conversations, where one can meet merchants looking for business, adventurers in search of work, and noble patrons seeking quiet indulgences.
