## Overview 

The Luminara Spire rises at the center of the [[Twilight Glade]], its pale stone and silvered wood catching even the faintest trace of starlight that filters through the canopy of the [[Endor Forest]]. Both temple and observatory, it serves as the spiritual and intellectual heart of the enclave devoted to [[Castor]].

The Spire is built in vertical harmony with the surrounding trees. Its lower halls are shaped from smooth stone fitted seamlessly into the basin’s natural foundation, while its upper levels transition into carved timber platforms that extend into the canopy. Open-air balconies and narrow observation decks ring the upper reaches, each carefully aligned to track specific constellations and lunar paths.

Within its interior chambers, polished brass instruments, star charts, and rotating celestial models fill quiet rooms lit by soft blue lanternlight. The priests and scholars who serve here, called Starguides, record stellar movements with meticulous precision. Generations of observation have made Luminara Spire one of the most reliable sites for tracking eclipses, meteor showers, and subtle anomalies in the night sky.
