## Overview 

Nestled within the [[Endor Forest]], just a day's journey east of [[Seabrook]], lies a small, lonely cabin nestled against the dense treeline. Once the home of a hunter, it has long since been claimed by [[Lucian]], the secretive sorcerer some locals whisper of as _the Witch of Endor_. Though isolated and unnerving in its aesthetic, the cabin serves as a safe haven for Lucian and his companions, containing books, supplies, and knowledge that may help the party's journey.
