## Overview 

Nestled between [[Seabrook]] and the western edge of the [[Endor Forest]], Seabrook Cemetery is a quiet field bordered by old stone walls and shaded by ancient trees. Wildflowers grow in clusters among mossy gravestones, and a crumbling statue of [[Castor]] stands vigil over the resting place of sailors, hunters, and old families. Though the cemetery sees little foot traffic from locals outside of funeral days, it is immaculately maintained. The graves here are marked not only by names, but by carved motifs of their trade: nets, bows, compasses, and smithing tools.

Most of the tombs are simple, but a few larger mausoleums, including that of the Drallion family and some of the founding fishers, sit closer to the rear tree line. A narrow, shaded path leads to a raised memorial stone for sailors lost at sea, and a circle of stones near the back.
