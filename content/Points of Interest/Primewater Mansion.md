## Overview 

The grandest private residence in [[Seabrook]], Primewater Mansion sits like a jewel at the edge of the harbor, a constant reminder of old money, elegant influence, and the fine line between respectability and suspicion. The mansion is home to Councilor [[Gellan Primewater]], the wealthiest man in town. Its lavish decor, foreign imports, and constant string of parties make it a social hub, for those invited.
