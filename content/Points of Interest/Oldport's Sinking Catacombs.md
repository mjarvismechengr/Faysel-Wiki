## Overview 

The sinking catacombs are small, self contained portion of [[Oldport]]’s sprawling catacombs, open to the surface and hidden behind some old boards. These catacombs hold the remains of the [[Hallwinter Family]], a now dead line of nobles known for their integrity and knightly attitudes. 

Recently, the [[Cult of Daghor]] took control of the sinking catacombs, using it as a base camp for their operations in the area. The Hallwinters had a key buried with them, which was recovered by the cult, and subsequently taken by the [[Seaward Milkmen]].
