## Overview 

Mirskwa was once among the greatest elven cities to rise after [[The Sundering]], a beacon of recovered arcane knowledge and bold magical innovation. Built where several leylines converged at the meeting of the [[Endor Forest]] and [[Goldwood]]. Its towers were grown and shaped rather than quarried, its streets traced in luminous stone that responded to spellcraft, and its scholars were said to rival even the archmages of the distant past.

Then something went wrong.

Accounts differ on whether it was arrogance, sabotage, or miscalculation, but a catastrophic arcane event tore through the leyline convergence at Mirskwa’s heart. The result was not a single explosion but a sustained magical collapse. Reality fractured, wild energies bled into stone and soil, and the city became the epicenter of a lingering arcane blight. The disaster did not simply destroy Mirskwa. It poisoned it.

Centuries later, the ruins remain unstable. Twisted spires lean at impossible angles, their surfaces etched with glowing fractures that pulse faintly at night. Streets are choked with warped vegetation that grows in unnatural patterns, and the air carries a subtle pressure that unsettles even seasoned mages. Strange magical phenomena flare unpredictably. Spells misfire. Light bends. Whispers echo without source.

The land surrounding Mirskwa bears the scar of the catastrophe. Flora and fauna in the immediate region have mutated under prolonged exposure to ambient arcane fallout. Creatures born of corrupted magic and entirely new abominations stalk the shattered districts. The closer one travels toward the city’s center, the more volatile the environment becomes.

Rumors of relics within persist. Mirskwa is believed to have recovered lost techniques from the [[Age of Wonder]] and created arcane devices unseen elsewhere in Faysel. Treasure hunters, scholars, and desperate factions have all attempted to probe its outskirts. Very few have ventured beyond the outer wards. Fewer still have returned, and those who did speak of accumulating magical sickness, unstable spell surges, and a growing sense that the city itself resents intrusion.
