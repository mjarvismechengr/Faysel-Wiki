## Overview 

Deep in the moss-laden interior of the Endor Forest lies the site of Bardock’s reawakening, the collapsed hillside that gave way during a recent storm, exposing long-buried remains. Here, pieces of Bardock's forgotten past lie, half-consumed by time and nature.
