## Overview 

The Sea Grove of Rambak is a quiet, sacred grove dedicated to [[Rambak]], the Oakfather, god of kinship and the wild. Located on the southern cliffs of [[Seabrook]], the grove offers a tranquil view of the [[Siren Sea]] and stands as a place of reflection, restoration, and quiet reverence.

Unlike the bustle of temples in larger cities, this grove is alive, not with crowds, but with the subtle presence of nature. It is common to see wild animals moving peacefully through the grove, especially birds, squirrels, and foxes. Locals visit to meditate, leave offerings, and share moments of peace with others. The faithful sometimes speak of a calming presence that dwells here, believed to be a sliver of Rambak’s will.

While Rambak’s followers maintain the grove, they do so with minimal intervention. The trees are never pruned, and no permanent buildings are allowed. Even the altar is a natural formation coaxed into being by generations of druids and rangers. The grove is also a place of sanctuary for lost souls, injured animals, or those in mourning.
