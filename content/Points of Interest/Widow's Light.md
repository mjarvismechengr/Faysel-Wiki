## Overview 

Rising above the bustling docks and tangled streets, [[Oldport]]’s lighthouse, known as the Widow’s Light, stands as the city’s tallest and oldest structure. Built from pale limestone, weathered by sea spray and time, its beacon casts a golden glow across the bay, guiding ships safely to harbor; or, as some say, warning them of the dangers that lurk in the mist.
