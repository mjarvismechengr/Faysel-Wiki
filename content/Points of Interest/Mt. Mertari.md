## Overview 

Mt. Mertari is the volcanic heart of [[Chinoshima Isle]], contested by the [[Dimaheim Horde]] and [[Shimazu Shogunate]]. Towering above dense pine forests, black volcanic cliffs, and ash-choked ravines, the mountain is a spiritual axis for two nations that worship [[Rodikar]] in radically different ways.

It is the birthplace of Rodikar, firstborn mortal after [[the Sundering]], and according to myth, the site where he ascended to divine glory. Since time immemorial, the mountain has been a beacon of conflict, prophecy, and supernatural activity.

The war over mountain has raged for centuries, sometimes shifting into Yoseju territory, sometimes pushed far into Dimaheim soil, but no matter how fluid the borders, the front always collapses back to this mountain.
