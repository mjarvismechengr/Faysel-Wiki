## Overview 

[[Kiorna Kester]] runs this tannery, where she produces smooth, colorful leather for every purpose and sells both the cured hides and items she fashions from it. Kiorna is keenly interested in acquiring hides from exotic creatures to craft into expensive leather. She pays a large fee in gold pieces for the intact hide of any beast or monstrosity that is sufficiently rare or dangerous.
