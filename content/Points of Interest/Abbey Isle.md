## Overview 

Abbey Isle lies several miles off the coast of [[Seabrook]] in the [[Siren Sea]]. From the water it appears as a low slab of rock rising from restless surf. Jagged shoals ring most of the island, and waves break hard against its cliffs. Only a narrow stretch of sand at the southern tip provides a safe place to land.

The island itself is sparse and wind-beaten. Grass and small flowering plants cling to shallow soil between exposed stone. A few stunted trees lean permanently away from the sea. There is little wildlife, and what exists feels incidental rather than native. The atmosphere is stark and isolated, shaped more by weather than by any guiding hand.

Near the center stand the burned remains of an abbey reduced to blackened stone and scattered debris. What survives above ground is little more than a foundation and a stair descending into intact cellars below. Those lower chambers once housed clerics and guards and were built with secrecy and defense in mind. Hidden passages, trapped corridors, and guarded vaults protected stores of wealth and ritual objects beneath the ruin.

Abbey Isle is remembered along the coast as a place of uneasy history. Pirates once dealt with the abbey’s clerics, and the island’s defenses included undead guardians seeded through the southern dunes. Though the structure has fallen, the events there revealed clear ties to the cult of [[Daghor Malatok]], marking the isle as one of the first confirmed coastal outposts connected to that wider movement.
