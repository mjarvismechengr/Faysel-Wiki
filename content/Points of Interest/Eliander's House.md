## Overview 

[[Eliander Fireborn]]’s house is a quiet, sea-facing refuge that reflects the man himself: disciplined, weathered, and built on strong convictions. Once a decorated soldier in the army of [[Kingdom of Thrane|Thrane]], Eliander retired after a serious injury to his leg defending [[Dimaheim Horde]] raids near [[Marshfield]]. He moved to Seabrook to live out his days in peace… only to be called back to service as captain of the town guard and now as a key member of the town council.
