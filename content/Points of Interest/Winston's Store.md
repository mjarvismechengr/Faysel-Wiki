## Overview 

Winston’s Store is a cluttered, low-ceilinged curiosity shop located near the [[Mariners' Guildhall (Seabrook)]], tucked between a cluster of fisher cottages. It sells a little of everything: gear for the road, tools for trade, and occasionally even relics dredged up from the Siren Sea or scavenged from the Endor Forest. The store has a reputation for being the place to go when you don’t know what you need—but you’ll likely find it anyway.
