## Overview 

The Oweland House is less a mansion and more a communal estate, a testament to the long-standing prominence of the Oweland family in Seabrook. Though the family is among the wealthiest in town thanks to their fishing fleet, the house remains open and welcoming to extended kin, trusted deckhands, and even struggling townsfolk between jobs. The halls are loud, the kitchen always smells of stew or fish pie, and there’s a perpetual sense of movement and hospitality.

The house is home to Matron [[Eda Oweland]], a member of the [[Seabrook Town Council]]. Though it lacks the elegance of the [[Primewater Mansion]], it makes up for it in soul. The Owelands consider themselves the heart of Seabrook, and they see their home as a bastion of the town’s traditions.
