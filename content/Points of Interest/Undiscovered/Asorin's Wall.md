## Overview 

Asorin’s Wall is one of the Ten Candles of the [[Candelabra]], serving as the guild’s miltary fortress just outside [[Orint]] in [[Yoseju]]. Built within the caldera of an active volcano, the tower rises from a rocky island surrounded by a lake of molten lava. It is the seat of [[Rhogar Nyxirius]], the Archmage of Defense, and functions as the Candelabra’s center for abjuration, evocation, fortifications, battlefield strategy, spellswords, and military-grade magical armaments. The tower is one of the most heavily defended places on the planet, reflecting its purpose as both a symbol of Candelabra authority and the guild’s answer to magical war.
