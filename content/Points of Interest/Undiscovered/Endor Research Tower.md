## Overview 

The Endor Research Tower is an isolated wizard's tower hidden deep within the [[Endor Forest]]. Though outwardly appearing to be little more than the residence of a powerful and reclusive spellcaster, the tower's contents reveal a far darker purpose. Its halls contain evidence of necromantic research, occult experimentation, and connections to the [[Cult of Daghor]], suggesting that its owner is far more than a simple scholar.

The tower is filled with libraries, laboratories, arcane devices, and carefully hidden secrets. Much of the research conducted within appears focused on death, undeath, ancient history, and the recovery of forbidden knowledge. While many questions remain unanswered, one thing is clear: whoever operates this tower possesses immense magical talent, considerable resources, and a role within the cult far beyond that of a common follower.
