## Overview 

The Sharkfin Bridge is one of [[Seabrook]]’s most iconic landmarks. Its broad, ancient stonework predates the modern town and spans the [[Roaring Brook River]] that feeds the harbor. It functions as a thoroughfare and a quiet gathering place, especially in the early evening, when the water reflects the sinking sun.

The bridge is also rumored to be cursed, especially among the elves and goblins of the region. Many claim they feel disoriented or uneasy when crossing it. While most locals dismiss it as superstition, some older residents swear there’s something not right with the stonework beneath, the way it seems colder than it should be, or the dreams it inspires after too many crossings.
