## Overview 

Shepherd's Highway is the longest and most connected road in [[Faysel]], stretching from [[Matroh]] in the south to [[Zoraskiv]] in the far north. It binds continents, kingdoms, and cultures into a single continuous artery of trade, diplomacy, and pilgrimage.

Beginning in Matroh, capital of the [[Kingdom of Esjiq]], the highway runs northward through major political and economic centers including [[Independence]], capital of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]], and [[Grenevik]], capital of the [[Dimaheim Horde]]. Along its length it passes through vital trade hubs such as [[Set-Kepesh]], [[Askandir]], [[Oldport]], [[Marshfield]], and [[Omnigrad]]. Grain, metals, timber, livestock, silk, and crafted goods move along it in constant flow, supported by caravansaries, customs stations, and fortified mile towns.

The road crosses nearly every kind of terrain found in the known world. It winds through desert and prairie, cuts across river valleys and marshlands, climbs mountain passes, and threads deep forests before reaching the frozen north. Some stretches are paved in fitted stone, others reinforced with gravel and timber foundations, but all are maintained due to their strategic and economic necessity.

Beyond commerce, Shepherd's Highway holds deep religious significance to the [[Shepherd's Vigil]]. The road represents guidance through darkness, both literal and spiritual. Walking the entire highway from Matroh to Zoraskiv is considered a sacred pilgrimage among the devout. Such journeys are undertaken not for speed, but for wisdom gained along the way.

Wayshrines dedicated to [[Castor]] stand at regular intervals across the highway and its connecting routes. These shrines serve as places of prayer and celestial observation, but also as practical sanctuaries. Many provide sheltered rest stops, lantern towers to mark treacherous bends, and raised platforms from which the stars can be read. In dangerous regions, the shrines function as quiet beacons that guide travelers safely through fog, mountain passes, and forest roads.
