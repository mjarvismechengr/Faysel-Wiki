## Overview 

Nestled near the [[Shepherd's Highway]] in [[South Seabrook]], the Carpenters' Guildhall is a marvel of joinery and ambition: a two-story timber structure built entirely without nails. Its seams are joined by clever dovetails, tight notches, and interlocking wooden pins, all sanded to buttery smoothness and varnished in hues of chestnut and amber. A carved archway marks the entrance, depicting a great tree whose branches curl into tools, scrolls, and a polished wand.

### Services 

The [[Carpenters' Guild]] in [[Seabrook]] provides a range of services to guild members, local residents, and shipwrights along the coast:

- **Custom Carpentry & Joinery:** Commission anything from furnishings and cabinetry to ornamental woodwork. Guild-certified artisans are available for projects both practical and decorative.
    
- **Repair & Reinforcement:** Specialists can reinforce damaged structures, mend storm-battered docks, or reframe warped walls, especially valuable after heavy rains or rough winters.
    
- **Shipwright Support:** Though not a full shipyard, the guild provides hull repairs, mast replacements, and interior woodwork for moored vessels, working in tandem with the Mariners’ Guild.
    
- **Apprentice Labor:** Affordable labor is available through supervised apprentices, ideal for small home projects or temporary scaffolding needs.
    
- **Tool Lending & Sharpening:** Members and trusted locals can borrow or sharpen saws, chisels, and hand tools for a modest fee or deposit.
	
- **Art:** Many locals, as well as travelers passing through often, often commission works of art ranging from small figurines to intricately carved statues and masts.
    
- **Timber Brokerage:** The guild purchases lumber from local trappers and sells milled planks to contractors and citizens alike, often favoring native woods like silverpine, red fir, and drift-oak.
