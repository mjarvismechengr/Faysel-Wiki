## Overview 

The Shattercrest Colossus stands in the [[Endor Forest]], carved directly into the sea cliffs where the [[Koryx River]] meets the [[Siren Sea]]. From across the water in [[Grenevik]], the statue is visible on clear days as a towering silhouette against sky and surf.

The monument depicts [[Iskandar]] with one arm raised toward the heavens, fingers spread as if drawing lightning from the clouds. His stance is forward and unyielding, cloak and hair sculpted in sweeping lines that suggest constant wind. The figure rises from the living stone of the cliff itself, its base fused seamlessly into the rock face above the crashing tide.

Storms gather frequently at the river’s mouth, and the statue is often framed by thunderheads rolling in from the sea. Sailors claim lightning strikes the raised hand more often than chance would allow. Whether coincidence or quiet blessing, the sight has only strengthened the Colossus’ reputation among coastal folk and river captains.

The Shattercrest Colossus serves as both landmark and warning. For ships entering or leaving the Koryx, it marks the transition between inland trade waters and open sea. For those on land, it represents the volatile meeting of river and ocean, forest and frontier, civilization and storm.

Though not an active temple, offerings sometimes appear along narrow paths that wind toward the cliff base. Fishermen leave charms for safe passage. Warriors carve small sigils into nearby stone.
