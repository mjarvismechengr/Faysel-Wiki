## Overview 

Andy’s is Bromjinar’s music shop, known as much for its instruction as its instruments. The shop specializes in the repair, tuning, and crafting of stringed instruments, though Andy’s real reputation comes from the musicians he’s taught over the years. Sheet music, both traditional and heavily annotated, fills the space, reflecting a practical, hands-on approach to music rather than academic theory. Many local performers got their start here, and Andy remains a quiet but respected figure in the town’s cultural life.
