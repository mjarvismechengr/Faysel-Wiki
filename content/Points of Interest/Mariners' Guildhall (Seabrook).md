## Overview 

The Mariners’ Guildhall in [[Seabrook]] is a rough-and-ready hub where captains, sailors, and coastal travelers gather to hire crew, swap news, or secure cargo. Though not as lavish as some guilds in larger cities, Seabrook’s hall is one of the most reliably organized and widely respected along the [[Siren Coast]].
