## Overview 

Seabrook Market lies in town square, and is the heart of the town’s daily rhythm and its economic soul. This open-air market sees a mix of local craftsfolk, farmers, and traders from the [[Shepherd's Highway]] and [[Siren Sea]]. On any given day, you might find a dwarven mason selling stone carvings, a silver-tongued halfling hawking rare jams, or a wandering merchant with half a wagon of curiosities and not a single name to go with them.
