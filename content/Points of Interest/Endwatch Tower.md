## Overview 

Endwatch tower stands high on a hill just outside [[Seabrook]] and overlooks the [[Endor Forest]]. This 60-foot-tall tower was the first defensive building of Seabrook, and it still serves as an armory and lookout as well as the official base of the town guard. Town guards take shifts in the watchtower keeping their eyes peeled for danger coming from the forest. 

While not fortified enough to withstand a siege, it functions more like a ranger station, scout garrison, and wilderness watchtower rolled into one. Its strategic perch allows it to oversee both the forest's edge and the [[Shepherd's Highway]].
