## Overview 

Chasmkeep is one of the Ten Candles of the [[Candelabra]] and the seat of the Archmage of Tomes, [[Kadmos Onasis]]. It stands on a high cliff above the [[Eldhollow River]] within the city of [[Liberty]]. What began as the tower of a solitary hedge mage was fortified during an ancient war, then expanded as the Candelabra took shape and claimed it as the guild’s intellectual stronghold.

Chasmkeep is both library and school. It houses the first copy of all research conducted across the Candles and serves as the largest open repository of lore in Faysel. Seekers from across the world travel here to study, petition for access, or offer service in exchange for eleven days of sanctioned research within its halls. Access is structured and deliberate, reflecting the guild’s belief that knowledge is powerful and must be managed carefully.

At the same time, Chasmkeep functions as the primary training ground for aspiring mages. Its colleges are modeled after the domains of the Candles, each guided by faculty who report to the Archmage of Tomes. Lecture halls, practice chambers, observatories, and scriptoriums fill the fortress complex, blending disciplined scholarship with arcane experimentation.

The Primary Tower of Kadmos Onasis rises from the rectangular keep like a crown of stone and glass. Around it spread the vast Library stacks, the School complex, the Pillars of Pedagogy, and the Seeker’s Court where petitions are heard and disputes settled. From the river below, the silhouette of the fortress dominates the skyline of Liberty.

Chasmkeep defines the city that surrounds it. Liberty has grown into a thriving hub of trade and culture, yet the presence of the keep shapes its identity. To many it represents enlightenment and opportunity. To others it stands as a reminder that the Candelabra gathers and guards more knowledge than any single kingdom.
