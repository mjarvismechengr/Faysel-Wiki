## Overview 

Partially supported by stilts driven into the harbor waters, this rickety tavern is purportedly a haven lowlifes. The owner, [[Kreb Shenker]], takes coin from anyone and asks no questions. Troublemakers are thrown out the door, over the railing, and into the reeking harbor. Characters looking to carouse find this the best place for a rowdy night of drinking and brawling. The town guard comes here only if called.
