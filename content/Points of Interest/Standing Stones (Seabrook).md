## Overview 

Two enormous runestones stand on [[Seabrook Island]]. The rumor is that in ages past, a siren was chained to the stones here and sacrificed by an evil human tribe as an offering to the sea and since then, the fishing in the region has flourished. The siren’s spirit was captured in the stones, and her captivating song continues to echo through the weave and draw fish to the area. The siren’s sisters and allies, among them a powerful djinn, have scoured the planes in search of her spirit for centuries.

In reality, the runes are not funereal, they’re arcane sigils of alignment and transposition. The stones are the remains of an ancient teleportation gate, left dormant for centuries. [[Keledek]], has named it an Echogate, and has begun reactivating it in secret.
