## Overview 

The Solmor House serves as the residence and business hub of [[Anders Solmor]], the youngest member of the [[Seabrook Town Council]], and heir to a once-prominent fishing dynasty. With his mother Petra’s death under mysterious circumstances a few years ago, Anders inherited both the family estate and its trade fleet.
