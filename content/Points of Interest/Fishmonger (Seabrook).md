## Overview 

The docks are Seabrook’s economic lifeblood—where merchant galleons and fishing boats alike compete for space along weathered piers, and the politics of power shift with the tide.

The Fishmongers’ Plants are a collection of processing buildings set close to the docks, where the town’s vast fishing hauls are cleaned, gutted, salted, brined, and prepared for sale or export. The area is vital to the local economy, employing hundreds of laborers—mostly locals who come from long lines of fisherfolk.

Each building is dedicated to a specific type of catch or preservation method, with tightly scheduled shifts and a hierarchy of crew bosses, barrel runners, and fish cutters. Despite the intense labor and pungent conditions, many locals take pride in their work here, seeing it as a continuation of family tradition and the foundation of Seabrook’s success.
