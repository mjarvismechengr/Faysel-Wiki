## Overview 

Boatbreaker Keep is Bromjinar’s seat of authority and its most defensible structure, overlooking the town and the nearby waters. It functions as both residence and meeting hall for [[Topal Boat-Breaker]], the town’s leader. The keep is not grand, but it is practical, designed to withstand raids, storms, and political pressure alike. During festivals and times of unrest, it becomes the center of decision-making, hosting negotiations that may shape Bromjinar’s future.
