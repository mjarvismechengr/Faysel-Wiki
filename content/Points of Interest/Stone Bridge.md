## Overview 

Stone Bridge spans the mouth of the [[Koryx River]] where it widens and empties into the [[Siren Sea]]. Forming a central stretch of the Shepherd’s Highway, it connects northern [[Isomar]] to the city of [[Grenevik]] in [[Dimaheim]]. It is the longest and most ambitious bridge ever constructed in the known world.

Unlike traditional arched crossings, the southern approach continues level from the cliffside road along the Koryx gorge, projecting outward over the river’s vast expanse. For miles the bridge runs level above frigid water and rolling fog before gradually descending in a monumental stone arc toward the northern shore. The structure stretches nearly ten miles from end to end, and about 500 feet above the water, its granite surface broad enough to support heavy trade caravans, military columns, and constant two way traffic without congestion.

The Koryx at this point is immense. Fed by glacial waters and inland tributaries, it surges toward the sea in a cold, relentless flow. The river’s width, depth, and winter ice make ferry crossings unreliable and seasonal. Stone Bridge transformed that vulnerability into permanence. By anchoring directly into the cliff faces and bedrock of both continents, it created the only dependable land crossing at the river’s mouth.

The bridge is built of fitted granite blocks, reinforced internally with dwarven engineering and supported by magic. Its width accommodates wagons side by side, mounted patrols, and defensive fortifications at key intervals. Guard towers and customs stations stand at either end, reflecting its strategic importance as both trade artery and political boundary.
