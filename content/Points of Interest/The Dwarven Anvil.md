## Overview 

The blacksmith’s forge has a single anvil with clear signs of dwarven origins, and a backlog of orders ten miles long. The human smiths make hooks, nails, harpoons, knives, fishing weights, and much more all day. Their master smith is an elderly, dark-skinned woman named [[Mafera]]; her son, [[Jasker]], is her best journeyman. A small shrine to [[Cyra]] can be found under the eaves as well, though it is somewhat neglected.
