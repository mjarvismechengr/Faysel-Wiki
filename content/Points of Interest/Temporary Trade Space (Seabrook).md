## Overview 

A trade delegation led by [[Xendros]]  has come to [[Seabrook]] to acquire large quantities of fish (salted and preserved for transport) in the name of [[Og'riq]], though few seem to know or care who that is. 

Though they claim to serve a “lord of order,” the Faithful Quartermasters of [[Og'riq]] are known more for their well-supplied inventories than for religious zeal. Their pavilion changes location every few seasons, but for now they’ve established a presence in Seabrook’s Market more for economic necessity than faith. The presence of Og’riq’s banner stirs little fear. In fact, many townsfolk see the pavilion as harmless and even quaint, a bizarre oddity in a corner of the world too provincial to draw true tyranny.

The trade space offers rare potions, scrolls, charms, and minor magic items, and occasionally deals in exotic goods from the frozen north. The guild accepts coin, barter, or favors in trade.
