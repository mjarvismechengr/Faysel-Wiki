## Overview 

Services at this long-standing temple are well attended. The congregation is led by a blind former whaler: [[Darius Brinehand]], an older human man with a sharp memory for every storm, lost ship, and enormous catch ever brought into [[Seabrook]]'s harbor. He knows many fanciful stories of shipwrecks, lucky escapes, and famous captains. Darius rarely ventures from the temple grounds, yet many visit him not for religious observance, but for guidance, seeking clarity amidst their personal tempests.

Though modest in appearance, the temple is spiritually vital to Seabrook’s identity. In a town where so many rely on the sea for livelihood, Iskandar’s favor is seen not just as divine, but as practical. Monetary offerings as well as offerings of sea glass, broken compasses, and driftwood tokens are common.
