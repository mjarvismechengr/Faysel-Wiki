## Overview 

Keledek Tower stands alone at the edge of Seabrook, perched on a rocky bank of the [[Roaring Brook River]] shrouded in mist. A modest three-story spire of stone and slate, it exudes the humble charm of a reclusive wizard’s abode. The tower is both home and arcane research station to Keledek, the mysterious and genteel wizard who has taken residence in Seabrook. Most of the town knows it as the place where “the curry smells come from."
