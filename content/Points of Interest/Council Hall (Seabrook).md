## Overview 

The Council Hall is the administrative heart of [[Seabrook]] and the seat of the [[Seabrook Town Council]]. Built from stone quarried from the coastal cliffs and timber from the edges of the [[Endor Forest]], it’s one of the town’s most fortified structures, less from need than from tradition. A wide, ivy-draped balcony overlooks the town square, often used for speeches and proclamations. A bell tower stands near, and is rung for various purposes. 

Though Seabrook is growing, its governance remains personal. Decisions are made face-to-face, and the people are not shy about shouting from the gallery.
