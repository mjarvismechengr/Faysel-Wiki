## Overview 

The Demonweb Pits are the 66th layer of the [[Abyss]] and [[Lolth]]’s domain, a nightmarish expanse of endless webbing suspended across a void of shadow and silence. It is not a single location, but a tangled network of vast strands, bridges, and hollowed caverns woven from divine silk, stretching farther than any mortal mind can fully comprehend. Some portions resemble the Underdark twisted into impossible shapes, while others feel entirely alien, as though they were never meant to be understood by mortal senses.

Gravity is inconsistent, direction is subjective, and distance is deceptive. A path that appears close may take days to traverse, while a single misstep can send a traveler spiraling into the abyss below, where nothing is known to return. The web itself is alive in subtle ways, responding to movement, carrying vibrations across great distances, and alerting unseen predators to the presence of intruders.

Lolth does not rule the Demonweb Pits from a throne or a fixed seat of power. She is present throughout it, her awareness carried along every strand. Her followers believe that to walk the web is to be constantly judged, every action observed, every hesitation noted. The Demonweb is both her domain and her instrument, a place where only the cunning survive and where even allies are never truly safe.
