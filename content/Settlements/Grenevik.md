## Overview

[[Grenevik]] is the only true city of the [[Dimaheim]] continent, standing where the [[Koryx River]] meets the cold edge of the northern coast. Built against tundra wind and sea spray, it is a hard walled port and river city with strong defenses, heavy gates, and a street plan shaped as much by mustering space as by commerce. Outsiders often expect Dimaheim to be nothing but scattered halls and roaming camps. Grenevik is the exception, a permanent place large enough to swallow tens of thousands and still feel like it was built for war.

Politically, Grenevik belongs to the [[Dimaheim Horde]] without being ruled by any single tribe. No chieftain sits a lasting throne here. Instead, the city is maintained by a coalition of sworn clans whose authority is rooted in mutual obligation rather than centralized law. Their purpose is simple and practical: keep the peace inside the walls, keep trade flowing, and ensure the city remains usable as a gathering ground when the tribes must speak with one voice. This arrangement makes Grenevik stable by Dimaheim standards, but never complacent. Power shifts through reputation and the ability to enforce consequences.

Culturally, Grenevik functions as Dimaheim’s neutral ground. It is a trade center where coastal raiders, inland hunters, rune carvers, and foreign merchants can bargain without instantly starting a feud. It is also a proving ground where disputes are resolved publicly and status is earned in front of witnesses. In a society that treats struggle as sacred, Grenevik provides structure for conflict so it does not consume the city outright.

Religiously, the city’s public life is steeped in reverence for [[Rodikar]]. This devotion does not read as pious quiet. It reads as ceremony, challenge, and the constant expectation that strength must be demonstrated and renewed. The oaths that hold Grenevik together are sworn in his name, and the clans that keep its peace treat the city’s neutrality as a duty rather than a convenience.

Grenevik is therefore both practical and symbolic. It is where the Horde trades, negotiates, and bleeds without collapsing into endless tribal war. For all Dimaheim’s independence and fragmentation, Grenevik is the place that proves the tribes can build something lasting when they choose to.
