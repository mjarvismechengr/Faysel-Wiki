## Overview

[[Zizhok]] is a small mountain village pressed between the western foothills of the [[Sky Razor Range]] and the choking treeline of the [[Padrich Woods]]. It is not marked on most maps, and those who do record it often place it slightly wrong.

The village clings to a narrow valley where the forest thins just enough to allow fields of rye and root vegetables. Timber houses with steep, slate roofs huddle close together, their upper stories leaning inward over muddy lanes. Chimneys smoke constantly. Wooden shutters are thick and reinforced. Nearly every doorway bears charms of iron, bone, or carved ash meant to ward against what hunts after sundown.

Zizhok’s layout reflects fear more than planning. Homes cluster around a stone chapel and graveyard at the village’s center. The chapel’s bell tower is the tallest structure in town, not as a symbol of pride but as an early warning. From its narrow windows, watchers scan the treeline at dusk. The cemetery is enclosed by iron fencing, each grave weighted with stone slabs and etched with protective sigils. No burial here is casual.

Life in Zizhok is shaped by the knowledge that the Padrich is not empty. Wolves howl too close. Livestock disappear. Pale shapes are sometimes seen at the edge of torchlight. Vampires and werewolves are not campfire stories but grim realities, and most families can name a relative who did not die cleanly. 

To the north, beyond a stretch of increasingly tangled forest road, the trees thin into a wall of unnatural mist. No one in Zizhok claims to know what lies within. Hunters who wander too far do not return. Traders who attempt to skirt the fog are found days later, if at all, wandering with no memory of where they have been. The mist is not treated as a frontier but as a sentence. It simply is, and it takes.

Economically, Zizhok survives on timber, charcoal, hardy crops, and occasional caravan trade from braver settlements to the south. The caravans never linger. There are no walls, no real defenses beyond pitchforks, hunting bows, and desperation. Its listed defenses would be considered pathetic by any organized force, and that truth is widely understood.
