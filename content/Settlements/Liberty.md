## Overview

[[Liberty]] is a large river-city set deep in the [[Endor Forest]], built on wooded hills where the [[Eldhollow River]] can actually be controlled. It is the capital of the [[Duchy of Vaelwyth]] and one of the most important inland strongholds of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]], not because it is pretty, but because it is positioned to hold the forest line and keep the Eldhollow corridor open.

The city’s skyline is defined by [[Chasmkeep]]. What began as a fortress raised during an ancient war became the seat of the [[Candelabra]]’s Archmage of Tomes, and from there Liberty grew outward—first as a garrison town, then as a trade hub, and finally into a true frontier capital. Liberty still feels like it remembers that origin. Streets climb steep grades, districts are layered like terraces, and the city’s strongest buildings are placed as if siege is a matter of “when,” not “if.”

Liberty’s economy is a marriage of road, river, and scholarship. Timber, charcoal, hides, and inland goods move along the [[Eldhollow Road]] and downriver in short navigable stretches, then disperse toward the coast. In return, the city draws manufactured goods, coin, and ambitious people: seekers hoping to earn time in Chasmkeep’s stacks, merchants chasing contracts, and soldiers who know the Endor does not forgive weak patrols. The result is a city that can feel surprisingly orderly for its location—guarded gates, disciplined watch rotations, and laws that are practical rather than performative.

Life here is shaped by constant proximity to power. The crown’s authority is real in Liberty, visible in banners, barracks, and the steady pressure of taxation and oversight. At the same time, Chasmkeep’s influence is its own gravity: students, scribes, professors, and warded courtyards change the city’s rhythm, and it is normal to hear rumor and research spoken in the same breath. Liberty is not a “wizard city,” but it is a city that has learned to live beside wizards without flinching.

Liberty is, above all, an anchor. If the Eldhollow corridor fails, the forest isolates settlements, and the kingdom loses its most reliable route through the Endor. So Liberty endures—strong-walled, heavily watched, and busy with the kind of work that keeps a frontier from becoming a borderless wilderness again.
