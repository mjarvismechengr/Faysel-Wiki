## Overview

[[Kalakoayan]] is the largest city on [[Aequitorra]] and the greatest port on the [[Blood Sea]]. It stands where the jungle exhales into salt air, its bright stone walls and stepped temples rising above a crescent harbor thick with ships, outriggers, and river craft from every corner of the continent. For most outsiders, Kalakoayan is not merely a destination. It is the threshold to the [[Jvarro Jungle]].

The city is built in tiers along low coastal hills, its districts divided by painted walls and broad causeways. Bright pigments cover nearly every surface. Murals of feathered serpents, sun disks, jaguars, and river spirits watch over markets and courtyards. Flowering vines spill from balconies. Water channels cut through certain wards, fed by rain basins and cisterns that capture the frequent tropical downpours. When storms roll in from the Blood Sea, the entire city seems to glisten.

Kalakoayan’s harbor is a controlled chaos. Merchant dhows from distant archipelagos sit beside pirate hulls flying fresh colors. Jungle barges heavy with hardwood, gold dust, rare dyes, obsidian, and caged beasts drift in from [[Tonatiuh Citadel]] at the mouth of the [[Itzapantli River]]. The line between trader and raider is often thin, and wealth changes hands quickly. The city’s influence extends far beyond its walls because nearly all legitimate trade between the interior of Aequitorra and the wider world passes through its docks.

Beyond the harbor, the markets are the city’s true heartbeat. Dinosaurs serve openly as beasts of burden, hauling cargo, turning millstones, and competing in sanctioned street races that draw massive crowds and reckless wagers. Spice vendors, jade carvers, featherworkers, and river guides crowd beneath woven awnings. Music and drumbeats carry through the heat. The air smells of fruit, smoke, salt, and wet stone.

At the city’s center rises a stepped ceremonial complex older than the current government, a reminder that Kalakoayan stands atop layers of history. Priests tend to sun shrines and rain altars, while merchant councils negotiate monopolies and maritime contracts in shaded halls nearby. Authority here is pragmatic. Power belongs to those who can secure trade routes, maintain harbor defenses, and keep the jungle from reclaiming the streets.

Despite its size, Kalakoayan remains a frontier city at heart. The [[Jvarro Jungle]] begins in earnest only a short march inland. Expeditions depart weekly along jungle roads and river channels, chasing rumors of ancient stone cities swallowed by green. Some return heavy with relics. Many do not return at all.

What sets Kalakoayan apart is scale and contradiction. It is the only true city on a continent that resists settlement. It is vibrant and wealthy, yet always one bad season away from flood, plague, or pirate siege. It is central to the world by geography, yet culturally insular and fiercely independent. To stand on its harbor walls at sunset is to see ships bound for every sea, and behind them, an ocean of green that does not care who rules the coast.
