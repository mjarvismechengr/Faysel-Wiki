## Overview

Seabrook is a remote coastal village on the [[Siren Coast]], where the western edge of the [[Endor Forest]] breaks into cliffs and wet harbors along the [[Siren Sea]]. The town sits at the mouth of the [[Roaring Brook River]], drawing its fresh water, mills, and fisheries from the fast current that cuts down through the trees before emptying into the bay. Seabrook’s livelihood is practical and coastal: fishing, timber, and the steady labor that keeps boats afloat and roads passable in a land that is always trying to reclaim them.

Politically, Seabrook is self governed. It is administered by the [[Seabrook Town Council]], a small elected body that manages taxation, civil law, and local defense. In normal times, that structure suits the town’s temperament. Seabrook has long preferred distance from crowns and high politics, relying on local consensus and familiarity rather than garrisons or noble oversight.

That preference is being tested. Seabrook lies in territory that sits outside firm royal control, and the stretch of coast and roadway around it has become increasingly vulnerable to banditry and piracy. The [[Kingdom of Thrane]] has taken a sharper interest in the region as trade suffers, offering protection and investment in exchange for formal incorporation. The proposal promises safer roads, expanded shipping, and a larger future for the port, but it also threatens to end the town’s independence in everything but name.

Seabrook is not yet in crisis, but it is no longer quiet. Outside attention is reshaping what the village must become, and the question of whether Seabrook remains a secluded community at the forest’s edge or a frontier port of Thrane now sits at the center of local life.
