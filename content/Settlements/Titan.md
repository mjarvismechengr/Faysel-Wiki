## Overview

Titan is the greatest dwarven city of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]], carved into the peak, [[Karag-Dûm]], within the [[Sky Razor Range]]. From the outside it reads as a fortress built into a mountainside. From within it is a layered metropolis of grand halls, ramps, and deep districts where entire neighborhoods are hewn directly from the mountain’s ribs. Strong gates and controlled approaches make Titan difficult to threaten, and its defenses reflect that reality. This is a city not only built to endure siege, winter, and time itself, but one built as a monument to craft itself.

Titan’s identity is inseparable from craft. Its quarries and forges supply Thrane with stonework, weapons, armor, and the everyday metal goods that keep an army and a kingdom functioning. Even its exports of ale and tools carry the same reputation: beautiful, precise, and made to last.

Politically, Titan is Barony within [[Embervein County]], ruled by Baron [[Nori Giantfinger]]. In practice, power in Titan is shared between the formal structures of rule and the practical structures of production. Guild decisions, forge output, and the control of skilled labor shape policy as much as decrees. The city’s interests are usually aligned with Thrane’s, but Titan expects respect, autonomy in internal affairs, and recognition of its role as an industrial heart rather than a distant vassal.

Religiously, Titan is the institutional center of the [[Hearthforged Communion]], and the city’s public life reflects that influence. The forge is treated as civic infrastructure and sacred space alike, and communal obligation is reinforced through rites that bless labor, family, and the responsibility to build rather than merely take. This does not make Titan gentle, but it does make it cohesive. In Titan, precise craft and opulent creation are a prayer, an expectation, identity of a people.

Like any great city, Titan carries contradictions beneath its pride. Its wealth and production attract influence, competition, and quiet criminal economies, and the [[Ring of Brass]] is known to operate in the shadow spaces that exist wherever goods, contracts, and desperation meet. Even so, Titan’s reputation holds. To most of Thrane and much of Isomar, Titan remains the benchmark by which dwarven craft is measured and the place where the mountain itself has been turned into a working city.
