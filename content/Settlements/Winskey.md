## Overview

Winskey is a small coastal town on the [[Siren Coast]]. It sits north of [[Seabrook]] and south of [[Grenevik]], serving as a practical stop on the long coastal run between Grenevik and [[Independence]], and as a waypoint along [[Shepherd's Highway]]. Most travelers do not come for Winskey itself. They come because the road, the sea, and the schedules say they should.

The town’s life revolves around its modest port and the mouth of the [[Brannach River]], which empties into the [[Siren Sea]] not far away. Small craft move timber, game, and inland goods downriver when the water is kind, then carry salt, tools, and imported necessities back upriver in return. The docks are built for utility, not spectacle. Warehouses, net sheds, and repair yards crowd the waterfront, with taverns and bunkhouses close behind.

Winskey feels like a threshold settlement. To the south, the coast is more settled and predictable. To the north, the sea grows colder, the routes thin out, and the political assumptions of Isomar start to fray. Its position makes it the northernmost coastal town of western Isomar, which also makes it the most frequent target for raiders out of [[Dimaheim]]. The attacks are not constant, but they are expected often enough that the town treats readiness as routine. Lookouts are posted. Boats are kept ready to scatter or run for shallows. People store valuables as if they might need to leave with minutes of warning.

The proximity of the [[Endor Forest]] adds to Winskey’s character. The woods press close to the roads, wet and watchful, and the riverbanks can reveal old traces after heavy seasonal rains. Hunters, cutters, and guides pass through town in quiet waves, and anyone who claims to have gone deep into the Endor gets listened to, even when no one fully believes them.
