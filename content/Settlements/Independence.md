## Overview

Independence is the capital of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]] and the largest city in the world, rising from the engineered waterways of the northern [[West Marsh]] along the [[Siren Coast]]. Built where marshland meets open sea across the [[Dixon Sound]] from [[Taunden Isle]], the city stands as both administrative heart and symbolic centerpiece of Thranish order. What was once unstable wetland has been drained, layered, reinforced, and disciplined into avenues, districts, ports, and stone foundations capable of supporting more than a million residents.

As the seat of [[House Solmara]] and the court of King [[Lytharion Solmara]], Independence functions as a monarchy in its highest form. Royal authority is expressed through a dense lattice of charters, courts, ministries, and sworn offices that reach from palace to parish. Feudal obligation flows inward from the duchies, and in turn law, taxation, and policy flow outward. The city’s bureaucracy is both its strength and its burden. Records are meticulous, decrees codified, and offices clearly delineated, yet progress can be slowed by the very systems designed to ensure fairness and accountability.

Religiously, Independence is anchored by the [[Church of the Regal Guardian]], whose grand cathedrals, halls of arbitration, and judicial sanctuaries stand prominently throughout the city. Oaths of office, treaties, and major civic rulings are routinely witnessed under the symbol of [[Cyrus]]. Nearly every major faith maintains a visible presence here, but the language of law and measured justice shapes the tone of public life. Order is not merely encouraged in Independence. It is expected.

Economically, Independence is unmatched. Grain, cotton, tobacco, textiles, and fish flow outward from the marshlands and surrounding plains, while lumber, stone, steel, gemstones, spices, and crafted arms pour in through its ports and caravan routes. Its markets rival those of any great fantasy metropolis, and almost any legal good can be found within its walls. Formidable defenses, layered walls, standing armies, and naval power ensure that this prosperity is guarded.

Independence represents Thrane at its most refined and most complicated. It is a city of law, spectacle, ambition, and scrutiny. Every decision made here echoes across the kingdom, and every weakness carries consequences far beyond its streets.
