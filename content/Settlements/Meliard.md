## Overview

Meliard is a small, hard edged mining town in the [[Iron Hills]], built where the prairie gives way to exposed stone and shallow ravines. It is loud in the day, dim at night, and always coated in dust. The town’s existence revolves around extraction and shipment. Ore comes out of the hills, gets sorted and smelted where it can, then moves south and east by wagon and rail toward larger markets.

Meliard is the headquarters of the [[Iron Hills Mining Co.]], which controls many of the active claims in the region and sets the pace of local life. When the company expands, Meliard swells with crews, teamsters, smiths, and camp followers. When a vein runs thin or a site is closed, the town empties fast, leaving shuttered storefronts and half finished buildings behind.

Its defenses are poor and its authority is practical. A few hired blades, a tired constable, and whatever the company chooses to pay for in a given season. Claim disputes, theft, and labor fights are common, and the hills around town hide plenty of abandoned shafts that make easy shelter for anyone avoiding the road.

Meliard is just north of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]]'s border along [[Ventura Highway]], which makes it a frequent stop for merchants, recruiters, fugitives, and anyone with business that needs to stay slightly out of sight.
