## Overview

Calyn Flickerfoot is a wood elf ranger of the [[Twilight Glade]], where she serves as both hunter and watchful guardian along the borders of the settlement. Fifty years older than her brother [[Altair Flickerfoot]], she grew into her role while he was still learning to read the constellations, and she was the one who first taught him how to move silently through undergrowth, and respect the living rhythm of the forest. Where Altair looks upward for guidance, Calyn keeps her eyes level with the treeline, attentive to threats that approach on quiet feet, though she often teases him for his fascination with celestial objects.

Calyn represents the grounded heart of her family. After the death of their father, Sorin Flickerfoot, she became a steady presence in a household strained by grief, anchoring Altair even as his path began to pull him beyond the forest. Within the Twilight Glade she is known less for mysticism and more for reliability, the sort of guardian who does not speak often of faith but lives it.
