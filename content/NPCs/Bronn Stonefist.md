## Overview

Bronn Stonefist is the percussionist of [[Three Dwarves Down]], a dwarf whose thunderous rhythms and easy laughter have made him a familiar face wherever the band plays. He was raised in Fonbohr among the Stonefist clan and spent his early adulthood in the mines before a collapse left him with a lasting limp and a conviction that something beneath the earth caused the disaster. Music became his way out, and he now treats the band as chosen family, more comfortable anchoring the performance than chasing the spotlight.

In dwarven settlements across Stonehearth, Bronn matters because he bridges two worlds that rarely speak kindly of each other: the miners who break their bodies for ore and the entertainers who carry news, rumor, and relief from town to town. He is quick to befriend working folk, quick to listen, and quick to worry when talk turns toward the mines of [[Fonbohr]].
