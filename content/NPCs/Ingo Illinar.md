## Overview

Ingo Illinar, often called Ingo the Drover, is a quiet resident of [[Seabrook]] known for arranging reliable guards along the [[Shepherd's Highway]], marines, and hired muscle for ships traveling the uncertain waters of the [[Siren Coast]]. Operating out of his modest home, he has built a reputation as a practical man who understands discipline, logistics, and the dangerous reality of the world. Those who work with him tend to note his steady judgment and the quiet authority with which he organizes crews.

Despite his growing usefulness to the harbor community, Ingo himself keeps a deliberately low profile. He avoids politics, rarely speaks about his past, and prefers the company of fellow veterans and sailors to that of merchants or councilors. One of the few locals who knows him well is [[Eliander Fireborn]], with whom he occasionally shares drinks and old war stories.
