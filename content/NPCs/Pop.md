## Overview

Pop is the longtime owner and keeper of the [[Salty Seaman]] in [[Bromjinar]], a tavern that serves as one of the village’s social centers. An elderly hobgoblin with a warm demeanor and a fondness for lively company, he has built a reputation as both host and confidant to the people who pass through his doors. Travelers, fishermen, and locals alike know the Seaman as a place where a good meal, a strong drink, and an open ear are never far away.

In his younger years Pop studied magic at [[Chasmkeep]], though he long ago traded academic pursuits for the quieter life of running a tavern. Even so, his curiosity for magic never faded. Over the years he has quietly shared what knowledge he could with those who showed interest, including [[Dotrice]], whom he encouraged to explore both storytelling and the arcane arts. Beneath the laughter and music that fill the Seaman’s common room, Pop remains one of Bromjinar’s constant figures, known for offering guidance, kindness, and the occasional bit of wisdom to those setting out into the wider world.
