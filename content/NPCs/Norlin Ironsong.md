## Overview

Norlin is the heart and soul of "Three Dwarves Down," his sweet and melodic tenor voice capable of soothing even the angriest crowd. With his beard adorned in golden beads and his warm, approachable demeanor, he’s a crowd favorite. He grew up singing in marketplaces and taverns, his unassuming roots keeping him humble even as the band’s fame grows. 

Norlin is the mediator of the group, tempering [[Torgrin Brightflame]]’s fiery ego and [[Bronn Stonefist]]’s occasional insecurities. His optimism and charm make him the emotional anchor for the band, but he’s yet to fully realize his own leadership potential.
