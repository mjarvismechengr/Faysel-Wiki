## Overview

Xendros is a tiefling quartermistress who oversees the [[Temporary Trade Space (Seabrook)]] pavilion established in [[Seabrook]] on behalf of the Faithful Quartermasters of [[Og'riq]]. Acting as both merchant and negotiator, she manages the delegation’s supply of potions, scrolls, charms, and other uncommon goods brought from distant regions. Her operation trades freely with the town, purchasing preserved fish and other resources while offering items rarely found in a settlement of Seabrook’s size.

Among locals, Xendros is regarded as an unusual but increasingly familiar presence in the market district. She conducts business with calm confidence, speaking carefully and observing more than she reveals. While she openly acknowledges her service to [[Og'riq]], she presents the connection less as devotion and more as a philosophy centered on order.

The pavilion she manages has become a curiosity within Seabrook. Its stock changes often, and travelers sometimes arrive seeking it out specifically, knowing that Xendros occasionally deals in goods from far beyond the [[Siren Coast]]. For adventurers and merchants alike, she represents both an opportunity for rare trade and a reminder that even quiet coastal towns can attract interests from distant powers.
