## Overview

Torch is the charismatic frontman of "Three Dwarves Down," known for his fiery stage presence and powerful, gravelly vocals. Clad in flame-patterned leather armor and a golden chain adorned with a ruby, he channels his passion into every performance. Hailing from the forges of [[Titan]], Torch rejected his clan's rigid traditions in favor of the exhilarating freedom of music. His rebellious spirit and penchant for flair make him the crowd favorite, though his impulsive nature often causes tension within the group.
