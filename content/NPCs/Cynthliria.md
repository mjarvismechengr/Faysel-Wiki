## Overview

Cynthliria the Fair is a respected healer of [[Palindrar]] and a devoted servant of the [[Order of the Threefold Sun]]. She is known for tending not only to the city’s own, but to travelers, refugees, and pilgrims who arrive at the Promise of Light seeking aid, counsel, or simple relief before continuing on their roads. Her work has made her a familiar name among those who pass through Palindrar’s gates with more wounds than coin.

Within Palindrar, Cynthliria is regarded as steady and public facing, a figure tied to charity and the daily practices of Solara’s faith rather than its politics. She is also the mother of Lucian, whose exile remains a quiet point of tension in how outsiders speak of the city’s discipline and mercy.
