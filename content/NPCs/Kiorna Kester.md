## Overview

Kiorna Kester is the proprietor of [[Kester's Leather Goods]], a tannery and leatherworking shop known for producing durable hides and practical equipment used by hunters, sailors, and travelers throughout the region. A disciplined craftswoman and careful merchant, she manages the business personally, overseeing both the curing of hides and the creation of finished goods ranging from simple straps and travel gear to finely worked leather armor and specialty pieces.

Kiorna maintains close relationships with hunters operating in and around the [[Endor Forest]], regularly purchasing pelts and hides brought back from the wilderness including [[Arthur Mourhan]]. She has a particular interest in rare or dangerous creatures, paying generous prices for intact hides that can be turned into unusual and valuable leather. Through these dealings she has become a familiar contact for many adventurers and woodsmen who pass through the city, valued as both a reliable buyer and a steady presence in the local trade network.
