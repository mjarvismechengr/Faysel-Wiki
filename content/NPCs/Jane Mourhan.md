## Overview

Jane Mourhan, known to her family as Aunt Jane, lives in [[Zizhok]], where she helped raise [[Arthur Mourhan]] and [[Eric Mourhan]] after the violent deaths of their parents. Living alongside her husband [[William Mourhan]], she provided the household stability that allowed the two boys to endure grief and grow into themselves in a village shaped by hardship, old fears, and the constant shadow of mysterious mists to the north. Where William taught through the hunt and the woods, Jane’s role was rooted in care, routine, and keeping a home intact when the world outside felt cruel.
