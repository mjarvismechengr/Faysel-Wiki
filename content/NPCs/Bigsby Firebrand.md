## Overview

Bigsby Firebrand is a young mountain dwarf smith currently working at [[War Ore]] in [[Titan]], where his talent for weaponcraft has begun to earn quiet recognition. The son of [[Durn Firebrand]], Bigsby grew up around ore carts and tunnel dust before trading pick and shovel for hammer and forge. His work carries the sturdy practicality of a miner’s upbringing, shaped for use rather than ornament.

As a child he traveled alongside the mining company and formed a close friendship with [[Rex Tinker]], bonding over mechanisms, stonework, and the promise of invention. Though the road carried them apart, those early years left a lasting mark. Now, as his skill grows, Bigsby has begun seeking collaboration with enchanters of the [[Candelabra]], hoping to see his blades and tools serve as vessels for both craft and magic.
