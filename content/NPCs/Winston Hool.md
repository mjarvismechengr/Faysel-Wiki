## Overview

Winston Hool is a halfling merchant who runs [[Winston's Store]] in [[Seabrook]]. His small, crowded shop is well known among locals and travelers alike as a place where practical gear, odd tools, and unexpected curiosities tend to appear side by side on the same shelf. Fishermen stop in for rope and hooks, sailors for spare equipment, and those venturing into the forest for whatever might prove useful beyond the harbor.

Winston himself is a pragmatic and unassuming figure who seems more interested in keeping his shop stocked than in becoming entangled in local politics or grand events. Years of trading with sailors, scavengers, and travelers have given him a wide assortment of goods and a steady sense for what people are likely to need next. In a town shaped by the sea and the nearby wilderness, Winston’s Store has quietly become one of Seabrook’s most dependable sources for the practical tools that keep people working and exploring.
