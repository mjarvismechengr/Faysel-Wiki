## Overview

Gargruth Mudhead is a duergar sorcerer who travels the roads of [[Faysel]] as one of the two constant companions of the renowned bard Cosham. Quiet, watchful, and rarely inclined toward conversation, he serves as both arcane support and practical protection during the trio’s long journeys between courts, cities, and roadside taverns. While Cosham gathers crowds and attention through song, Gargruth tends to remain at the edge of the firelight, observing the room with a wary patience born of life beneath beneath the world's feet.

Despite his grim demeanor, Gargruth has proven a steadfast presence beside both Cosham and the leonin warrior [[M'aiq]]. His command of magic and disciplined temperament make him a dependable guard when travel leads through dangerous roads or uncertain territory. Those who encounter the group often remember Cosham’s voice first, but seasoned travelers quickly notice the silent duergar who watches everything and forgets very little.
