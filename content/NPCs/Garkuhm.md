## Overview

Garkuhm is a towering goliath who travels beside the wandering merchant [[Patches]] of Moonlight, serving as both companion and protector aboard the enchanted wagon known as the [[Magic Patch]]. Standing well over seven feet tall, he carries himself with the quiet confidence of someone long accustomed to danger. Though he speaks little, his presence alone is often enough to discourage trouble when the wagon rolls into unfamiliar towns.

Once an adventurer in his own right, Garkuhm now devotes his strength to safeguarding Patches and the rare goods that travel with him. He handles the practical realities of life on the road, watching the treeline when the wagon camps and standing close when negotiations grow tense. While Patches charms crowds with stories and trade, Garkuhm remains the steady shadow at his side, a silent reminder that the Magic Patch is not as defenseless as it might first appear.
