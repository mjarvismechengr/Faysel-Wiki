## Overview

Kraddok Stonehorn serves as the jailer of the [[Seabrook Jail and Barracks]], overseeing the holding cells where the town’s guards detain smugglers, brawlers, and other troublemakers who pass through the coastal settlement. A disciplined and physically imposing man, Kraddok approaches his work with a strict respect for rules and procedure, believing that order in a small but busy port depends on consistency more than force.

Before taking up his post in Seabrook, Kraddok served alongside [[Eliander Fireborn]] in the Thranish military, a connection that helped bring him to the town guard when the need for a reliable jailer arose. While Eliander manages the broader responsibilities of keeping the peace, Kraddok ensures that the jail itself runs without incident, maintaining the routines that keep prisoners contained and guards accountable.
