## Overview

Aubreck Drallion is a human merchant whose fortunes have risen and fallen with the tides of the [[Blood Sea]]. Once counted among the wealthiest traders of [[Seabrook]], he built his name on long-haul spice routes that stretched south toward the jungles of [[Aequitorra]] and the markets of [[Kalakoayan]]. A disastrous investment and the loss of his flagship reduced him to a far humbler station, forcing him to rebuild from what little remained.

When the wreck of his lost vessel was discovered, Aubreck turned to capable adventurers to recover the documents that represented his remaining claim to wealth. With their success, his standing was restored enough for him to settle his affairs in Seabrook, gifting his aging mansion in gratitude before departing for Kalakoayan to renew his trade in person. His return to the southern markets signals that the Drallion name may once again become a force along the equatorial routes.
