## Overview

Vertheg is the long-serving butler and household steward of [[Drallion Mansion]], known in [[Seabrook]] for his neat appearance, careful manners, and steady reliability. Once employed by the merchant [[Aubreck Drallion]], he remained with the estate through its decline and eventual transfer, continuing to keep the old house in working order even as its ownership changed hands. His loyalty has always seemed directed less toward wealth or status than toward the simple principle of seeing a household properly maintained. In many ways, he serves as the thread connecting the house’s former life under Aubreck to its new role under the [[Seaward Milkmen]].
