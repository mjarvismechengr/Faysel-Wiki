## Overview

Punketah serves as the deck mage aboard the [[Sea Ghost]], where his magic supports the smuggling runs of [[Sanbalet's Gang]] along the [[Siren Coast]]. Unlike the ship’s rougher sailors, he keeps a colder distance from crew and passengers alike, preferring to watch from the rigging or the edge of a conversation. His role is practical and dangerous, maintaining favorable winds, supporting the ship in moments of crisis, and lending the crew an arcane edge when quiet trade turns violent.

Among those who have crossed paths with the Sea Ghost, Punketah is remembered less for open threats than for the way he speaks as if he already knows more than he intends to share. He belongs to the same careful culture of professional secrecy that defines the vessel itself. On a ship where trust is rare and curiosity is often punished, Punketah embodies the guarded, quietly dangerous discipline that keeps Sanbalet’s operations moving.
