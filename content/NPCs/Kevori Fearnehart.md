## Overview

Kevori Fearnehart is an investigator operating in [[Oldport]]. Practical, disciplined, and driven by a strong sense of duty, she often works the quieter corners of the city where missing persons, smuggling routes, and old secrets intersect with the docks and catacombs beneath Oldport.

Her name became known to the [[Seaward Milkmen]] after the murder of her brother, Delvin Fearnehart, who had been assisting with an investigation into a series of disappearances near the harbor. Seeking answers, Kevori turned to outside help, setting events in motion that led to the discovery and destruction of a hidden cult cell operating in the city’s forgotten tunnels. Though the cult was broken, the investigation revealed hints of a wider network tied to older and darker forces, leaving Kevori with the sense that the work in Oldport is far from finished.
