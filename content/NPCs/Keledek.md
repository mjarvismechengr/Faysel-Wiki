## Overview

Keledek is a wizard from the [[Kingdom of Esjiq]] within the vast [[Maru Desert]]. He's spent years studying ancient magic, lost histories, and the strange structures that dot the world. He carries himself with the calm confidence of someone who has spent a lifetime pursuing knowledge others overlook. Though he once held connections within powerful magical circles such as the [[Candelabra]], he now operates largely on his own, traveling where his research leads him.

In recent years, Keledek has taken particular interest in ancient teleportation gateways, known as echogates, and other relics of the [[Age of Wonder]] scattered across the world. His work has brought him to places like [[Seabrook]], where he studies the standing stones near the coast and the forgotten magic that still lingers in the region. Rumor around town claims that speaking his name aloud allows Keledek to eavesdrop on a conversation for a short time.
