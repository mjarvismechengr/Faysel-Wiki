## Overview

Ogmund was one of the brutal enforcers stationed on [[Abbey Isle]] in service to the [[Cult of Daghor]]. Built for intimidation, he represented the sort of crude muscle the cult relied on to hold ground, guard its stores, and answer threats with direct violence.

His presence on the island underscored that the abbey was not merely a place of worship, but a fortified outpost with armed defenders prepared to kill for its cause. Ogmund’s death at the hands of the Seaward Milkmen marked the end of one more violent servant of Daghor, but it also revealed how deeply the cult had embedded hardened soldiers within even its smaller coastal cells.
