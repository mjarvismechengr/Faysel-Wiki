## Overview

Krell Grohlg was a half orc druid and member of the [[Cult of Lolth]] who took control of the drifting vessel [[Emperor of the Waves]] after its crew was scattered and killed during a disastrous voyage. From within the ruined ship he gathered various spiders to his side, turning the abandoned vessel into a dangerous lair as it drifted through the coastal waters.

His control of the ship ended when the adventuring company known as the [[Seaward Milkmen]] boarded the Emperor during a salvage operation. In the fighting that followed, Krell was killed by [[Bodhi Orsimer]], bringing an abrupt end to his brief and violent hold over the vessel.
