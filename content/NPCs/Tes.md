## Overview

Tes is a respected seamstress and tailor in the village of [[Bromjinar]]. She runs a modest workshop from her home, mending sails, clothing fishermen, and producing durable garments suited for life along the coast.

She is the mother of [[Dotrice]] and [[Wally]], and the wife of [[Beckett]]. Tes helped raise her children in a home defined by stability, encouragement, and strong values. While she never fully understood Dotrice’s fascination with songs, stories, and wandering adventurers, she never discouraged it either, believing that curiosity and creativity were simply different kinds of craft. Her influence instilled in him a sense of discipline and respect for the people whose lives fill the stories he now tells. Though she remains in Bromjinar, Tes continues to follow news of her children whenever it reaches the village.
