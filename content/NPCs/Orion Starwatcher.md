## Overview

Orion Starwatcher is a respected elder of the [[Twilight Enclave]] in the [[Twilight Glade]]. A devout follower of [[Castor]] through the traditions of the [[Shepherd's Vigil]], Orion is known for his wisdom and long study of the night sky. His life has been devoted to interpreting the movements of the heavens and guiding younger members of the enclave in the philosophies tied to the stars.

Among those he mentored most closely was [[Altair Flickerfoot]], whom Orion helped guide after the death of Altair’s father, [[Sorin Flickerfoot]]. While Orion shares the glade’s reverence for celestial guidance, he is known for approaching such matters with patience and pragmatism, reminding his students that the stars may illuminate a path but cannot walk it for them. His teachings helped shape Altair’s understanding of the cosmos and ultimately encouraged the pilgrimage that carried him beyond the borders of the glade.
