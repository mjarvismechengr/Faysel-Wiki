## Overview

Bulak Tanck is one of the three elder matriarchs who govern the [[Dramsfjord Enclave]] from [[Arvidsjaur]]. A half orc by heritage and a fixture of the fjord by reputation, she is known for plain speech and a willingness to challenge consensus when she thinks caution is being mistaken for wisdom.

Bulak helped raise [[Bodhi Orsimer]] within the enclave alongside [[Ghorza Kuisma]] and [[Olga Kokko]], and she treats that bond as family rather than duty. Though the Enclave’s worship of [[Rodikar]] is more restrained than many Dimaheim clans, Bulak maintains ties to the Bloodbound Legion and speaks comfortably in the language of war when the wider Horde calls.
