## Overview

Sorin Flickerfoot was a historian of the [[Twilight Enclave]] who devoted much of his life to studying celestial omens and the ancient history tied to them. Quiet, thoughtful, and often absorbed in his work, he was a familiar sight in the observatories of the [[Twilight Glade]], where he spent long nights recording the movements of the heavens and comparing them with stories preserved in old elven records. Many considered him brilliant but eccentric, a scholar whose fascination with the stars sometimes made him seem distant from the everyday life of the enclave.

To his son [[Altair Flickerfoot]], Sorin was both teacher and inspiration. He introduced Altair to the practice of mapping constellations and interpreting the subtle patterns that believers in the [[Shepherd's Vigil]] saw as signs from the cosmos. Their nights spent stargazing from [[Luminara Spire]] fostered Altair’s lifelong belief that the heavens held guidance for those patient enough to listen.

Sorin’s life ended unexpectedly after he contracted a mysterious illness while researching the ancient [[Ruins of Mirskwa]]. Despite the efforts of the enclave’s healers, the sickness could not be identified or cured. His death left unanswered questions and a lingering sense that the ruins held secrets beyond what he had been able to uncover. For Altair, the loss became a defining moment, deepening his fascination with fate, the stars, and the hidden forces that shape the world.
