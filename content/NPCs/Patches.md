## Overview

Patches of Moonlight, more often simply "Patches" is a wandering Tabaxi merchant of mystery and magic. Beneath his affable charm lies a master of the arcane arts, a former adventurer who has chosen to walk a quieter, though no less profound, path. His fur is black save for a striking white crescent over one eye, and his gear is eccentric yet elegant, heavily enchanted and worn with the confidence of someone who has _earned_ every scar.

He is a storyteller above all else, using tales to teach, mislead, inspire, or distract. He trades in magical items, arcane lore, potions, scrolls, and relics, some of which he makes, others which he acquires through mysterious means. Patches travels the world in a magically expanded wagon, _The Magic Patch_, accompanied by [[Garkuhm]], a quiet but formidable Goliath, and former adventuring companion.
