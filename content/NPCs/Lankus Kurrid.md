## Overview

Lankus Kurrid is the proprietor of [[The Wicker Goat]], the oldest tavern in [[Seabrook]] and a gathering place favored by guards, dwarven miners, and veterans of the [[Thrane|Thranish]] military. A former officer himself, Kurrid runs the establishment with the same steady discipline he once brought to the field, keeping the tavern orderly.

Kurrid purchased the Wicker Goat after retiring from military service, acquiring the property when its previous owner could no longer keep the business afloat. Though the transition was not without resentment from some locals, he kept the existing staff in place, including [[Thessa Brewthorn]], who continues to serve as the tavern’s primary keeper. Under Kurrid’s ownership the Goat has remained a stable fixture in town life, its smoke darkened rafters and old traditions drawing regulars who prefer familiar company and a quiet place to share stories from the road and the sea.
