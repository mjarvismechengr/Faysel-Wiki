## Overview

Gwen Tinker was a kobold mason and part of the wandering mining camps of the [[Iron Hills Mining Co.]] near [[Meliard]], where she lived alongside the kobold miner [[Glick Tinker]]. Their time together was brief but lasting, and their son [[Rex Tinker]] grew up hearing stories of her warmth and spirit even after she disappeared from the camps. While Rex remembers her only faintly from childhood, Gwen has continued her life elsewhere, carving out a place for herself far from the shifting tunnels and uncertain fortunes of the Iron Hills.
