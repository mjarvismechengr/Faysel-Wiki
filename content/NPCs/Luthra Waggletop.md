## Overview

Luthra Waggletop is the [[Candelabra]]’s Archmage of Artifice and one of the most accomplished magical engineers in the known world. From the [[Orrery of Brass]], she oversees research into constructs, enchanted items, mechanized systems, and the blending of spellcraft with precise physical design. Among artificers, smiths, and arcane craftsmen, her name carries enormous weight, and many of the guild’s advances in magical engineering are tied in some way to her work.

Unlike archmages whose influence is felt through diplomacy or secrecy, Luthra’s reputation rests on invention. She is associated with the practical transformation of magic into tools, weapons, engines, and durable systems that can shape everyday life as much as warfare or scholarship. Within the politics of the Candelabra she is often linked to those who push for aggressive innovation, making her a controversial but undeniably important figure in the guild’s ongoing struggle over how knowledge should be used.
