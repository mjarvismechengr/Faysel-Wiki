## Overview

Padmaja is an aasimar scholar and spiritual mentor based in [[Palindrar]], the radiant city often called the Promise of Light. He has long been associated with circles of study that question rigid interpretations of doctrine and encourage deeper exploration of the forces that shape the world, though publicly he condemns all heresy. 

Among his most notable students was [[Lucian]], whose unusual interests and relentless questioning set him apart from many of his peers. Padmaja recognized that same restless intellect and guided Lucian toward deeper study rather than suppressing it.
