## Overview

Rhogar Nyxirius is the [[Candelabra]]’s Archmage of Defense and master of [[Asorin's Wall]], the guild’s volcanic fortress outside [[Orint]] in [[Yoseju]]. Despite the title, his authority includes far more than protection: he oversees abjuration, evocation, fortifications, battlefield strategy, and the Candelabra’s spellswords. Rhogar is known as a disciplined, militaristic commander who treats magic as a tool of control and consequence. He is respected for his strategic mind, overwhelming power, and refusal to confuse caution with weakness.
