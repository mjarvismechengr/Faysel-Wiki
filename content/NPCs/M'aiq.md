## Overview

M'aiq is a leonin warrior who travels the roads of [[Faysel]] as one of the two constant companions of the renowned bard [[Cosham]]. Clad in heavy armor and carrying a large sabre, he serves as the bard’s visible shield, keeping order around a figure whose songs can draw crowds, curiosity, and danger in equal measure. Though often remembered first for his imposing presence, M'aiq is known among seasoned travelers as a disciplined guard whose watchfulness rarely lapses.

He was raised among the [[Mwindaji Pride]] of [[Al'Dira]], and he carries that culture’s emphasis on honor and earned strength into his life abroad. In contrast to Cosham’s public warmth and Gargruth’s quiet reserve, M'aiq tends to meet strangers with measured confidence and a dry manner that can leave others uncertain whether he is joking, testing them, or both. Wherever Cosham performs, M'aiq helps ensure that the stories are remembered for the right reasons, and not for whatever trouble might have interrupted them.
