## Overview

Wally is the younger brother of [[Dotrice]] and one of the many young people in [[Bromjinar]] whose life is shaped by the sea. Still in his teens, he already works as a fisherman and sailor, contributing to the steady rhythm of labor that keeps the village fed and connected to the wider coast. Where Dotrice was drawn toward stories, songs, and the larger world beyond the village, Wally seems more at home in Bromjinar’s working life, tied to its boats, tides, and daily routines.

Like the rest of his family, Wally comes from the close-knit social fabric that defines Bromjinar, where everyone is known and every absence is felt. His relationship to Dotrice helps ground the bard’s story in something ordinary and lasting: a younger brother still living the life Dotrice left behind.
