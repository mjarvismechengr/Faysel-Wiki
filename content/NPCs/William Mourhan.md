## Overview

William Mourhan is a seasoned hunter from [[Zizhok]] and the uncle who raised [[Arthur]] and [[Eric Mourhan]] after the death of their parents. William has long made his living by reading the wilderness rather than forcing it, tracking game through the nearby forests and teaching others how to survive in a region where mistakes can be fatal. Arthur learned much of his discipline and skill under William’s guidance, spending long days in the woods of the [[Padrich Woods]] learning to track, hunt, and move through the wild with care.
