## Overview

Thessa Brewthorn works as the primary barkeep of [[The Wicker Goat]], the oldest tavern in [[Seabrook]]. Friendly, patient, and quick with a welcoming smile, she has become a familiar face to the tavern’s regular crowd of guards, sailors, and old soldiers. While the Goat’s owner, [[Lankus Kurrid]], maintains the discipline and order of the establishment, it is often Thessa who keeps the place feeling warm and lived in, remembering regulars’ drinks, settling small disputes, and making newcomers feel less like strangers.

The tavern once belonged to Thessa’s family before financial hardship forced her father to sell it. When Kurrid purchased the property after retiring from military service, he chose to keep Thessa on staff, recognizing both her experience and the goodwill she carried with the local community. Though she harbors no open bitterness toward the arrangement, she quietly holds onto the hope that one day she might reclaim the Wicker Goat and restore it to her family’s name.

Despite that private ambition, Thessa’s nature remains generous and optimistic. She treats the tavern not just as a workplace but as a gathering place for people who spend much of their lives at sea or on the road. To many regulars, she represents the welcoming heart of the Wicker Goat itself.
