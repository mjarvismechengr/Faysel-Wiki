## Overview

Olga Kokko is one of the three elder matriarchs who govern the [[Dramsfjord Enclave]] from [[Arvidsjaur]], serving alongside [[Ghorza Kuisma]] and [[Bulak Tanck]]. Where Ghorza is associated with steady leadership and Bulak with blunt challenge, Olga is part of the quieter strength that keeps the fjord community intact. 

Within the enclave, Olga is tied as much to kinship as to governance. She helped raise [[Bodhi Orsimer]] after he was brought into Dramsfjord, treating him as her own family. Her presence helps define the enclave’s character: a people shaped by hardship and the old martial traditions of the north, yet governed with a measure of care and responsibility that sets Dramsfjord apart.
