## Overview

Sylvaris Flickerfoot is a healer of the [[Twilight Enclave]] who lives within the quiet sanctuaries of the [[Twilight Glade]] deep in the [[Endor Forest]]. Devoted to the teachings of the [[Shepherd's Vigil]], she practices a careful blend of traditional elven medicine, spiritual guidance, and patient observation of the natural world. Within the enclave she is respected for her steady hands and calm judgment, tending to the sick and injured while preserving the older healing traditions passed down through generations.

She is the widow of the historian [[Sorin Flickerfoot]] and the mother of [[Altair Flickerfoot]]. After Sorin’s death from a mysterious illness contracted at the [[Ruins of Mirskwa]], Sylvaris withdrew somewhat from the warmth she once showed her family. Though still dutiful in her role as healer and caretaker of the enclave, she became more reserved and distant, carrying her grief quietly.
