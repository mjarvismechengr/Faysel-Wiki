## Overview

Lytharion Solmara is the reigning monarch of the [[Kingdom of Thrane]] and head of [[House Solmara]], ruling from [[Independence]] as both king and chief guardian of the realm’s legal and political order. Widely respected as a disciplined and principled ruler, he is closely associated with Thrane’s ideals of justice, duty, and stability, and his reign has come to define the kingdom’s modern identity.

Lytharion inherited a realm marked by internal strain and lingering noble ambition, and much of his rule has been shaped by the long work of strengthening royal authority after the [[War of the Fractured Crown]]. In the years since, he has become known as a steady and serious king who places the needs of Thrane above personal comfort, relying on law, oath, and careful governance rather than spectacle. His close ties to the [[Church of the Regal Guardian]] and the institutions of the crown reinforce his image as a monarch who sees rule not as privilege, but as responsibility.

He is the known weilder of [[King's Blessing]], one of the [[Relics of Sundering]], further emphasizing his apparent divine right to rule.
