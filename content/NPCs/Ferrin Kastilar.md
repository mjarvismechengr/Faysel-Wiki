## Overview

Ferrin Kastilar is the halfling druid who tends the [[Sea Grove of Rambak]] on the southern cliffs of [[Seabrook]]. A quiet and somewhat melancholy figure, he lives simply among the trees and wind shaped stone of the grove, caring for the shrine with little interference in the natural rhythms that define it. His constant companion is a large bullfrog named Lorys, often found resting nearby as Ferrin walks the grove’s winding paths or watches the sea below.

Though gentle in demeanor, Ferrin maintains a vigilant watch over the surrounding wilderness. Through old ties with elves of the [[Twilight Glade]] and travelers who pass along the coast, he keeps informed of unnatural creatures or disturbances moving through the wilds. When such threats appear, Ferrin quietly seeks out capable hunters or adventurers willing to deal with them, believing that the balance of the natural world must be protected before danger reaches Seabrook or the sacred ground of the grove.
