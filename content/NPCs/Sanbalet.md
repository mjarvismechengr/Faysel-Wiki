## Overview

Sanbalet is the calculating leader of [[Sanbalet's Gang]], operating out of the abandoned [[Alchemist's House]] near [[Seabrook]]. A tall, sharp-featured wizard with a shaved head and piercing green eyes, he presents himself with theatrical confidence and a taste for dramatic villainy. Sanbalet delights in the role of mastermind, preferring manipulation, negotiation, and carefully staged intimidation over brute force. His success as a smuggler stems as much from his charisma and planning as from his arcane ability.

Originally drawn to the ruined house by rumors of the long-dead alchemist who once lived there, Sanbalet hoped to uncover forgotten magical secrets hidden within the structure. Though those discoveries never materialized, he quickly recognized the location’s strategic value. The house’s isolation, hidden passages, and proximity to the coast made it an ideal hub for smuggling operations moving contraband through the [[Siren Coast]].

Sanbalet’s ego is nearly as large as his ambition. He frequently speaks of himself in the third person and treats criminal enterprise as a kind of performance, casting himself as the brilliant villain at the center of a grand scheme. Beneath the theatrics, however, is a cunning and opportunistic operator who understands when to negotiate, when to threaten, and when to let others do the dangerous work on his behalf.
