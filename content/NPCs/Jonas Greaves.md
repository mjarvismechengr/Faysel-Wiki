## Overview

Jonas Greaves is the long serving keeper of [[Widow's Light]], the ancient lighthouse that rises above [[Oldport]] and watches the bay through fog, storm, and long stretches of silence. Once regarded as a capable sailor and dependable warden of the tower, he is now known more often as a gaunt recluse who rarely leaves his post and speaks to visitors only in warnings, fragments, or uneasy riddles. Many in Oldport assume solitude has hollowed him out, though others suspect the tower itself has given him reason to fear what waits below.

Despite his reputation, Jonas remains bound to the work of the light. He tends the beacon with obsessive care, watches the sea as though expecting some long delayed return, and refuses to abandon the tower even when common sense would urge it. To the people of Oldport, he is part of Widow's Light as surely as the stone and glass, a living reminder that the lighthouse is more than a landmark and that some old duties are carried as much by dread as by devotion.
