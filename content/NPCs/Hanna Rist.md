## Overview

Hanna Rist is the proprietor of [[The Snapping Line]], a well known dockside inn and tavern in [[Seabrook]]. Built from the salvaged hulls and timbers of old fishing vessels, the establishment reflects the life of the harbor. Sailors, fishers, and travelers alike pass through her doors, trading stories and news over drink while the scent of the sea lingers in every beam.

Though still young, Hanna is regarded as a reliable presence along the waterfront. She keeps a watchful eye on the rhythms of the docks and the moods of the crews who frequent her tavern, making The Snapping Line a place where locals gather not only for food and drink, but for conversation and the latest rumors carried in from the sea.
