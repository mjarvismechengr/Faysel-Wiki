## Overview

Eliander fought in the royal army of [[Kingdom of Thrane|Thrane]], where he earned a name driving the wild things of the [[Endor Forest]] away from the settled lands. Eliander suffered a tremendous injury to his leg in a battle against an owlbear and now walks upon a finely carved wooden peg. Despite his injury and advancing age, the burly captain of the Seabrook town guard remains an imposing figure. 

Eliander is a local celebrity thanks to his facility with languages, and he is often called upon by the town’s various organizations to assist with translations. He is fully literate in Common, Dwarvish, Gnomish, Elvish, and Halfling; he can also speak and understand Orc and Draconic.
