## Overview

Loz is a towering goliath who serves as both the bouncer of the [[Salty Seaman]] and an informal guardian of the village of [[Bromjinar]]. Known for his calm demeanor and formidable presence, he keeps order in the tavern and lends his strength to the community when trouble arises. Locals trust him as someone who values fairness and protection over intimidation.

Originally raised among a small giant tribe near the forests at the base of [[Nayru Mountain]], Loz spent his youth learning to fight as part of the tribe’s competitive traditions. As he grew older he chose to leave that life behind and seek a place among the smaller folk of the coast. Bromjinar became his home, where his love of seafood, honest work, and quiet camaraderie made him a familiar and respected figure among the villagers.

Loz played an important role in the early life of [[Dotrice]], teaching him the fundamentals of swordplay and encouraging his confidence beyond storytelling and music. Before Dotrice left Bromjinar to pursue adventure, Loz gifted him a rapier as a parting token, a gesture of friendship that reflected the bond the two had built within the tavern halls of the Salty Seaman.
