## Overview

Decimus Ogmarr is a towering storm giant who once fought as a gladiator in [[Bloodlust Arena]], where his name carried weight long before freedom did. Known for his disciplined brutality and controlled presence, he trained enslaved fighters not in spectacle, but in survival, shaping raw strength into deliberate force. Among those he mentored was [[Bodhi Orsimer]] of the [[Dramsfjord Enclave]], whose skill and resolve were forged under Decimus’s watchful eye.

Unlike many giants bound in chains, Decimus carried himself with the bearing of someone who had not surrendered inwardly. When revolt came, he did not hesitate. His leadership during the uprising against their captors marked him not only as a warrior, but as a figure others were willing to follow. Though his path since the arena remains largely unknown, his influence endures in those who survived because of what he taught them.
