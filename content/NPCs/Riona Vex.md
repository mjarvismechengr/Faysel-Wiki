## Overview

Riona Vex is the guildmaster of the [[Mariners' Guild]] in [[Seabrook]], a seasoned seafarer and administrator known for her discipline, directness, and refusal to waste lives on poorly planned ventures. From the guildhall she oversees contracts, shipping concerns, and coastal safety, treating maritime work as a matter of order and responsibility rather than romance. Her support for projects such as securing [[Abbey Isle]] and improving navigation along the [[Siren Coast]] reflects the Guild’s broader interest in stabilizing the waters around Seabrook.
