## Overview

Eda Oweland is a [[Seabrook]] merchant and veteran member of the [[Seabrook Town Council]], known for speaking plainly and backing her words with work. A lifelong local and devoted supporter of the town’s fishing families, she pushes for Seabrook’s independence and measures every outside offer by what it costs the people who live here. When she is frustrated she swears like a sailor, and she tends to meet newcomers with suspicion until they prove they can keep their heads and stand their ground.

Eda’s influence comes from old relationships and practical authority, not polish. She is quick to volunteer aid when the town is in trouble, and quick to challenge anyone she thinks is trying to buy Seabrook’s future. In council matters she often represents the voice of tradition and the working waterfront, making her an important figure in the ongoing debate over Seabrook’s direction and who it chooses to answer to.
