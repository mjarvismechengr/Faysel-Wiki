## Overview

Gellan Primewater is the wealthiest merchant in [[Seabrook]] and a prominent member of the [[Seabrook Town Council]]. Known for his impeccable dress, refined manners, and generous patronage of festivals, music, and public celebrations, he has cultivated a reputation as a cultured benefactor who delights in bringing color and excitement to the town’s otherwise hard working coastal life. His mansion, lavish entertainments, and constant support of public events have made him one of the most recognizable figures in the community.

Behind this polished image stands a merchant whose influence is built on decades of careful trade and shrewd alliances. Gellan’s shipping interests move goods along the [[Siren Coast]] and beyond, helping connect Seabrook to larger markets across the region. On the council he typically aligns with the traditionalist faction, supporting the town’s independence and the interests of its merchant families. Though he often plays the role of an indulgent host more interested in wine and music than politics, few in Seabrook doubt that Gellan Primewater understands exactly where power and profit intersect.
