## Overview

Wolgar Windrune is a dwarven captain and seasoned sailor who commands [[The Soul of Winter]], a ship known along the [[Siren Coast]] for its reliability and disciplined crew. A practical leader who values preparation and clear agreements, Wolgar has built a reputation among merchants and expedition leaders as someone who takes contracts seriously and sees them through without unnecessary drama.

The crew of the Soul of Winter reflects Wolgar’s approach to command. Most are experienced dwarven sailors and former guards accustomed to strict watches and orderly routines, creating a vessel that runs with the efficiency of a well drilled unit. Under his leadership, the ship has become a dependable option for transporting goods, passengers, or adventurers bound for dangerous work along the coast.

Wolgar is not known for grand speeches or flamboyant personality, but his judgment carries weight among those who deal with him. The voyage that carried the [[Seaward Milkmen]] to the wreck of the [[Emperor of the Waves]] only reinforced his reputation as a captain willing to face risk so long as the contract is clear and the ship is prepared for what lies ahead.
