## Overview

Hogaak is an ancient earth elemental titan said to lie buried somewhere within the depths of the [[Endor Forest]]. Scholars, druids, and storytellers across [[Faysel]] speak of the being as a creature formed of living stone and soil, a remnant of [[The Founding]], when elemental forces shaped the world more directly. Over time, forest and earth are believed to have grown over its resting form, leaving only rumor and scattered accounts to mark its presence.

Most people regard Hogaak less as a monster and more as a dormant natural force. The concern surrounding the titan lies not in cruelty or intent, but in sheer scale. Should such a being ever awaken, the land itself would shift with its movement, and the forest, rivers, and settlements nearby would feel the consequences. For this reason, the stories of Hogaak persist as a quiet warning tied to the deep wilderness of the Endor Forest, where the oldest powers of the world are said to sleep beneath the soil.
