## Overview

Odium was a low-ranking priest of the [[Cult of Daghor]] who was present on [[Abbey Isle]] when the outpost was attacked and burned. Frail, ill-tempered, and more concerned with his own survival than with loyalty or courage, he was remembered by the other survivors as a constant complainer whose devotion did little to make him useful in a crisis. His presence at the abbey reflected the island’s role as one of the cult’s active cells, tied to the movement of goods, relics, and forbidden texts along the [[Siren Coast]].

Though minor in station, Odium still mattered because he represented the sort of petty, desperate servant the cult often relied upon: ambitious enough to involve himself in dangerous work, but self-serving enough to turn on his fellows when it suited him. His death on Abbey Isle left behind only fragments of what he knew.
