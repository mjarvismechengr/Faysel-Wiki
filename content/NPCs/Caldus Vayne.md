## Overview

Caldus Vayne, is the leader of the [[Gilded Compass]], a disciplined adventuring company that operates along the [[Siren Coast]]. A Knight of the [[Order of Saint Kyren]] and a veteran of the [[War of the Fractured Crown]], he carries himself like a knight even when he is not serving a crown, holding to a strict personal code and expecting professionalism from those who take contracts under his banner. In guildhalls and dockside taverns, his name is associated with clean work, clear terms, and a reluctance to look the other way when a job turns ugly.

To [[Seabrook]] and its surrounding coast, Caldus matters because he represents a competing standard for what an adventuring company can be. Where most sellswords chase coin and excuses, the Gilded Compass builds a reputation on restraint and follow-through, which makes them welcome to cautious patrons and frustrating to those who thrive on disorder. As rivals to other crews moving through the region, Caldus is the sort of leader who forces comparisons, not through boasting, but through results.
