## Overview

Ozymandias was the senior surviving cleric of the [[Cult of Daghor]] on [[Abbey Isle]] after the outpost was shattered by attack and fire. A careful, status-conscious man rather than an inspiring one, he held authority through rank and ritual rather than force of personality. In the aftermath of the abbey’s fall, he tried to preserve what remained of the cell’s order and secure passage back to the mainland before the island’s losses became complete. His death, like the abbey’s ruin, marked the collapse of one more piece of the cult’s wider network along the [[Siren Coast]].
