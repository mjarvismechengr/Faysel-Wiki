## Overview

Glick Tinker was a kobold miner who worked the shifting excavation camps of the [[Iron Hills Mining Co.]] near [[Meliard]]. Known among his fellow laborers for his steady hands and inventive mind, he believed that clever tools and patient craftsmanship could make even the harsh life of the mines a little easier. Many small contraptions that circulated through the camps began as his quiet experiments.

He was also the father and early mentor of [[Rex Tinker]]. During their years traveling from site to site, Glick encouraged Rex’s curiosity about mechanisms, ancient inventions, and the long lost traditions of arcane engineering. The two spent much of their free time building small devices from spare parts and scrap metal, turning the rough camps of the Iron Hills into workshops of improvised creativity.

Glick’s life ended in a mine collapse during one of the company’s operations, an event that left lasting scars among the miners and deeply shaped Rex’s path. For those who remember him, Glick is recalled not only as a capable worker, but as someone who believed that even in the darkest tunnels, a clever mind could still bring light.
