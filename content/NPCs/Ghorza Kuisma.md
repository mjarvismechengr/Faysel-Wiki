## Overview

Ghorza Kuisma, known throughout the [[Dramsvol Fjord]] as the Dramsfjord Matriarch, serves as the head of the ruling council of the [[Dramsfjord Enclave]] in [[Arvidsjaur]]. An elderly half-orc whose authority rests as much on experience as on strength, she has guided the enclave for many years alongside the other matriarchs, [[Bulak Tanck]] and [[Olga Kokko]]. Within the traditions of the [[Dimaheim Horde]], the Dramsfjord council is unusual, favoring steady governance and consensus over the singular rule common among other clans.

Among her people, Ghorza is respected for her ability to balance the demands of the wider Horde with the quieter needs of the fjord. She values stability, the health of the enclave’s families, and the preservation of the land that sustains them. Beyond politics, she is also remembered as one of the elders who helped raise [[Bodhi Orsimer]] after he was brought to the enclave as a child, treating him not as a ward but as one of their own.
