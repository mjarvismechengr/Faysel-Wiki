## Overview

Zalryr was the leader of a clandestine cell devoted to [[Daghor Malatok]] operating beneath [[Oldport]] in the ancient tunnels known as [[Oldport's Sinking Catacombs]]. Under his direction, the group carried out disturbing rituals aimed at extracting knowledge from unwilling victims, leaving behind a trail of disappearances that eventually drew attention within the city’s dockside districts.

The cult’s activities were uncovered when the [[Seaward Milkmen]] investigated a murder connected to the case of [[Kevori Fearnehart]] and her brother. Following the evidence into the catacombs, the party dismantled the cult’s operation and confronted Zalryr at the center of the hidden complex. Rather than killing him, they captured and interrogated him, extracting fragments of information about the cult’s activities and its broader interests in ancient sites and sealed powers by cutting off his hand and feeding him his own fingers. Afterward they handed him over to [[Kevori Fearnehart]].
