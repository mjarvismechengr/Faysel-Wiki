## Overview

Beckett is a farmer of [[Bromjinar]], known more for steady hands and quiet patience than for grand ambitions. He has spent most of his life tending the same fields that fed his parents before him, measuring the years in harvests rather than milestones. In a village where everyone knows everyone, Beckett’s reputation is simple and solid. He works hard, speaks plainly, and keeps his word.

As the father of [[Dotrice]] and [[Wally]], Beckett raised his sons in a home where curiosity was never punished and stories were never dismissed as foolishness. Though his own life remained rooted in the soil of Bromjinar, he never begrudged Dotrice’s desire to chase songs beyond the village horizon. Those who know Beckett understand that while he may not travel far, his influence travels with his children, carried in the steadiness he taught them.
