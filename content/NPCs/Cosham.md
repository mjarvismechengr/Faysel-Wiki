## Overview

Cosham is a world renowned bard whose performances have shaped how much of [[Faysel]] remembers its own history. An elderly drow who walks openly beneath the sun, he is instantly recognizable in his ornate robes. Though age has bent his posture, his voice remains clear and commanding, and his recollection of past wars, fallen heroes, and political turning points is said to rival that of any archive.

He travels the roads with two constant companions, the leonine warrior [[M'aiq]] and the duergar sorcerer [[Gargruth Mudhead]], and rarely remains in one settlement for long. In taverns, courts, and roadside camps alike, Cosham trades stories, preserving them in song before they are lost to time. His ballads of the [[Battle of Quoridon]] are especially famous, inspiring younger performers such as Dotrice of Bromjinar to seek lives worthy of remembrance. Wherever he appears, crowds gather not only for entertainment, but to hear history given breath.
