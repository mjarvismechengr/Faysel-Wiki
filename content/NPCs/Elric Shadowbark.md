## Overview

Elric Shadowbark serves as the senior council member of the [[Twilight Enclave]] in the [[Twilight Glade]], where he is regarded as a steady, pragmatic voice in matters of governance and survival. A wood elf of disciplined temperament, he places his trust in tangible action, trained rangers, and careful stewardship of the [[Endor Forest]] rather than in celestial interpretation. To Elric, the glade’s safety depends on vigilance and preparation, not on reading patterns in distant stars.

Though a follower of the [[Shepherd's Vigil]], Elric’s faith expresses itself through duty and structure rather than mysticism. His skepticism toward omens has brought him into quiet conflict with [[Altair Flickerfoot]], whose departure from the enclave he viewed as impulsive and misguided.
