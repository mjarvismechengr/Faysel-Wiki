## Overview

Eric Mourhan is a young man from [[Zizhok]], raised in the shadow of hardship and loss. After the murder of his parents, he and his brother [[Arthur Mourhan]] were taken in by their aunt and uncle, [[William Mourhan]] and [[Jane Mourhan]], and grew up under the steady discipline of a hunter’s household. Where Arthur turned inward and hardened, Eric retained a gentler disposition, shaped more by community and faith.

A devotee of the [[Shepherd's Vigil]], Eric believes in quiet wisdom and the protection of home and kin.
