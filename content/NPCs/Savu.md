## Overview

Savu is a rakshasa from the [[Nine Hells of Baator]] who operates as a broker, intermediary, and facilitator of information. Rather than possessing every answer himself, Savu specializes in finding the individuals, organizations, artifacts, or sources capable of providing what his clients seek. He maintains an extensive network of contacts spanning multiple planes and appears to trade as readily in favors, secrets, and opportunities as he does in gold.
