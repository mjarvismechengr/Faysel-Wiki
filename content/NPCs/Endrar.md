## Overview

Endrar the Bold serves as a political advisor within the high council of [[Palindrar]]. Though he holds no formal title beyond his advisory role, his voice carries weight in the Promise of Light, where reputation and piety matter as much as rank. A devout member of the [[Order of the Threefold Sun]], he embodies the disciplined, orderly devotion expected of those who serve Solara’s radiant vision.

Recognized throughout the city for both his celestial heritage and measured counsel, Endrar represents Palindrar’s ideals of structure, faith, and civic responsibility. As the father of [[Lucian]], his public standing was tested by his son’s exile, a matter that quietly reshaped his place within the city’s social and political circles. Even so, he remains committed to preserving Palindrar’s stability and the authority of its sacred traditions.
