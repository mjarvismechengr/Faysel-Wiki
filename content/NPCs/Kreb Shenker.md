## Overview

Kreb Shenker is the proprietor of the [[Empty Net]], a rough harbor tavern built on creaking stilts above the water where sailors, dockworkers, and less reputable visitors gather for drink and distraction. The establishment has a reputation for rowdy nights and hard brawls, and Shenker runs it with a simple rule: anyone with coin is welcome, but anyone who causes the wrong kind of trouble finds themselves thrown out into the harbor below.

Within Seabrook’s dockside circles, Shenker is known as a man who keeps his business quiet and his eyes open. The Empty Net often serves as a meeting place for sailors looking for work, merchants seeking discreet arrangements, and travelers passing through the port who prefer their conversations unrecorded. While the town guard rarely intervenes unless summoned, Shenker maintains his own kind of order, ensuring that the tavern remains lively but never completely out of control.
