## Overview

Durn Firebrand is a seasoned miner with the [[Tin Tunnels Mining Co.]], a broad shouldered mountain dwarf whose life has been shaped by stone, sweat, and steady work. Based out of the [[Tin Tunnels Mine]], he is known as a dependable hand who values consistency over ambition and takes pride in the honest labor that feeds forges and workshops.

A devoted follower of the [[Hearthforged Communion]], Durn views mining as both livelihood and calling, a craft that demands respect for the earth and for those who break it open. He is the father of [[Bigsby Firebrand]], and though his son has traded the tunnels for the forge, Durn’s influence remains visible in the practical, durable nature of Bigsby’s work.
