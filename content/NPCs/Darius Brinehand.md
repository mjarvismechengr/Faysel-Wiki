## Overview

Darius Brinehand is the elderly priest who keeps the [[Temple of Iskandar (Seabrook)]], a weathered place where sailors, dockhands, and storm shaken travelers come to leave offerings and hear plain spoken counsel. He is known for his steady presence and for treating hardship as part of the coast’s bargain, not a personal failing, and the temple’s small rites often serve as the closest thing Seabrook has to comfort that does not ask questions.

Though he is devoted to the Brotherhood of the [[Brotherhood of the Crashing Wave]],most of his influence comes from years of listening to the town’s troubles as much as from any sermon. Lucian has found unexpected friendship here, and Darius is one of the few in Seabrook who speaks to him without flinching, offering guidance that feels earned rather than given.
