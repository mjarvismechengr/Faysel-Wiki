## Overview

Jasker is a journeyman blacksmith at [[The Dwarven Anvil]] in [[Seabrook]], where he works under the watchful eye of his mother, the master smith [[Mafera]]. Strong, energetic, and already highly skilled with hammer and tongs, he has earned a reputation among local sailors and fishers as a reliable craftsman capable of turning out sturdy tools and blades for life along the coast.

While Mafera’s work favors clean lines and strict dwarven technique, Jasker’s style shows a more expressive touch. He enjoys adding decorative flourishes to otherwise practical pieces, a habit that sometimes earns a sharp word from his mother but also draws attention from customers who appreciate a bit of artistry in their gear. Despite the occasional disagreement, the two share a close working partnership, and Jasker is widely regarded as the smith most likely to carry on the reputation of the forge.
