## Overview

Lirielle Vaust is the proprietor of [[The Silver Lantern Inn]], one of the most prominent and well regarded establishments in [[Oldport]]. Known to many simply as Silver, she runs the inn with a practiced ease that balances hospitality, discretion, and careful observation of the many travelers who pass through its doors.

Before becoming an innkeeper, Lirielle spent years moving within the higher social circles of port cities as a high class courtesan, a background that left her well connected and adept at navigating conversation as easily as commerce. Under her management the Silver Lantern has become a place where merchants negotiate deals, adventurers search for opportunity, and visiting dignitaries seek privacy away from the noise of the docks. Lirielle herself maintains a reputation for charm and sharp perception, and those who frequent the inn understand that while the atmosphere may feel relaxed, little escapes her notice.
