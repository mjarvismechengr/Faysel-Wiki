## Overview

Bloody Bjorn is the first mate of the [[Sea Ghost]], a veteran sailor who keeps the ship running through hard discipline and hard-earned routine. He serves with [[Sanbalet]]’s smuggling operation, acting as the practical hand on deck when deals are made and cargo is moved, and he is known among the crew for being gruff but consistent. For all his scars and reputation, Bjorn’s authority aboard ship comes from fairness as much as intimidation, and the crew tends to follow him when tensions rise.

In [[Seabrook]] waters, Bjorn is the sort of man most decent folk try not to notice until they have to, but his name travels anyway, carried by dockside talk and the quiet fear that follows smugglers. Those who meet him up close often find he is more readable than his nickname suggests, especially when he decides someone has earned plain treatment.
